# 🧘 Monk

> *The body is the temple; the fist is the bell.*

**Archetype:** Spirit

The Monk surrendered every possession at the cloister door and kept only breath, bone, and discipline. Years of fasting, stillness, and ten thousand repetitions forged the body into an instrument that needs no blade and fears no siege. Some temper the flesh until steel means nothing; others learn the breath beneath the flesh and strike what armor cannot cover. Both roads end in the same silence.

## The Tree

```text
Monk (Lv 1)
├── Iron Vessel (Lv 10)
│   ├── Unmoved Pillar (Lv 25) → Stillgate (Lv 40) → Seated Mountain (Lv 60)
│   └── Idolbreaker (Lv 25) → Palm of Ruin (Lv 40) → Iron Knell (Lv 60)
└── Breathbinder (Lv 10)
    ├── Pilgrim of Nowhere (Lv 25) → Roadless Saint (Lv 40) → Tenth Step (Lv 60)
    └── Hollow Hand (Lv 25) → Heart-Stiller (Lv 40) → Empty Heaven (Lv 60)
```

## Tier I — Initiate (Level 1)

### Monk

**Role:** Melee striker — unarmed, fast, self-reliant

*Every mountain cloister keeps a novice-roll of those who arrived with nothing and asked to be less. Village orphans, deserters, disgraced scholars — the gate does not ask. They sweep, fast, and repeat the First Form ten thousand times, learning that the body, properly instructed, is the only possession that cannot be taken.*

**Playstyle.** Chain short unarmed strikes into rhythm, weaving between blows; you have no gear to break and no weapon to lose, only tempo.

**Taking the first step:**

1. Arrive at a cloister gate carrying nothing; whatever you still own will be burned on the threshold.
2. Kneel outside for three days without asking entry; the door opens for patience, never for knocking.
3. Surrender your name to the novice-roll and answer only to the work you are given.
4. Repeat the First Form until your hands finish it in your sleep, then begin learning it properly.

**Abilities granted:**

- **First Form: Falling Water** — A three-hit open-hand sequence; each strike lands faster than the last and refunds a sliver of stamina.
- **Measured Breath** — Spend a still moment to steady the lungs, cleansing one minor ailment and restoring focus.
- **Open Guard** — Deflect the next weapon strike with a bare forearm, taking reduced damage and no stagger.
- **Beggar's Footing** — Move at full speed across rubble, ice, and rooftops; poverty taught you every surface.

## Tier II — Adept (Level 10)

### Iron Vessel

**Role:** Frontline bruiser — armorless endurance · **Advances from:** Monk

*At the tenth year some novices are led to the cold hall, where the outer discipline begins. Iron Vessels stand beneath winter falls, take the staff without flinching, and cure their skin like bell-bronze. Soldiers mock the bare chest exactly once. The cloister sends them where the fighting will be worst and longest.*

**Playstyle.** Wade in unarmored and stay there; convert punishment into steadiness, trading flourish for the certainty that you will still be standing.

**The road to Iron Vessel:**

1. Reach level 10 and stand the Trial of the Ford: remain in the winter river until the ice accepts you.
2. Take one hundred staff-blows from your teachers without stepping back, counting each aloud so the count never wavers.
3. Sleep a full season on bare stone without fire, and rise each morning without complaint.
4. Shatter the cured oak striking-post with your forearm, then stand where it stood for a day.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Bellbronze Skin** — Years of tempering grant flat damage reduction against all physical harm; you wear no armor and need none.
- **Root Below Root** — Plant your stance; you cannot be knocked down, dragged, or thrown while your feet hold the earth.
- **Swallowed Thunder** — Absorb a blow's force into the breath and release it through your next strike as added weight.

### Breathbinder

**Role:** Mobile skirmisher — ki bursts, closing distance · **Advances from:** Monk

*Others are led inward, to the breathing hall where nothing moves but air. Breathbinders learn that breath is the hinge between body and spirit, and a hinge can be swung. They snuff candles at nine paces, cross rooms in a stride too short to see, and speak rarely, hoarding the breath.*

**Playstyle.** Dart in, spend stored breath on sudden force, and be gone before the counterswing; your resource is patience made explosive.

**The road to Breathbinder:**

1. Reach level 10 and hold a single breath through the whole of the Night Litany, from first bell to last.
2. Extinguish nine candles from nine paces with one exhalation, disturbing none of the smoke.
3. Cross the gorge bridge blindfolded, placing each foot where your breath says the rope will be.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Bound Breath** — Hold and compress a breath over several heartbeats, storing force you may release through any later strike.
- **The Shortened Road** — Step once and arrive several paces away, crossing pits, flames, and spears as if they were rumor.
- **Exhaled Edge** — Release a focused breath as a cutting lash of force, striking a target beyond arm's reach.
- **Reed in Current** — Bend around an incoming blow without moving your feet, letting it pass through where you almost were.

## Tier III — Specialist (Level 25)

### Unmoved Pillar

**Role:** Anchor tank — zone denial, punishing patience · **Advances from:** Iron Vessel

*Garrisons petition the cloister for an Unmoved Pillar the way they petition for grain. One stands where the line must not break — a gate, a ford, a stair — and the line does not break there. They do not pursue. Whatever comes to move them becomes, briefly, an education in why nothing does.*

**Playstyle.** Claim a piece of ground and make it law; enemies who test you are held, slowed, and returned their own violence with interest.

**The road to Unmoved Pillar:**

1. Reach level 25 and hold a single doorway against all comers from dusk-bell to dawn-bell, yielding not one step.
2. Sit the Long Vigil: forty days at the cloister gate, moving only for breath, while pilgrims mistake you for statuary.
3. Stand in the path of a charge of horse and be the reason it becomes a halt.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Stance of the Last Door** — While stationary, enemies cannot pass through your reach; the first each round that tries is stopped cold.
- **Weight of Stillness** — Your calm presses outward; enemies within reach strike slower, as if moving through deep water.
- **Returned With Interest** — Store a portion of damage taken while rooted and deliver it back through your next palm strike.
- **Patience of Stone** — Each round you do not move, gain stacking damage reduction, kept until you take a step.

### Idolbreaker

**Role:** Armor-breaker striker — demolition of the fortified · **Advances from:** Iron Vessel

*When a false god's shrine must come down and no army can be spared, the cloister sends one Idolbreaker with empty hands. They study a thing until they find where it lies about its own strength, then strike there once. Stone idols, warded doors, champions in proofed plate — everything has a fault.*

**Playstyle.** Read the target, wait for the opening, then commit everything to a single decisive strike that ignores what the enemy trusted most.

**The road to Idolbreaker:**

1. Reach level 25 and split a millstone with one bare palm, on the first and only strike you are allowed.
2. Enter a shrine of a devouring god and leave nothing standing above knee height, taking no relic for yourself.
3. Break a champion's shield, then his sword, then his nerve, and let him walk away to say so.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Sutra of Breaking** — Your strikes treat armor, shields, and stone as if they were unfired clay, ignoring physical hardness.
- **Fault-Finding Eye** — Study a target briefly to reveal its structural flaw; your next strike against that flaw cannot be mitigated.
- **Third Knuckle** — Land a blow that seems gentle; the true force arrives three heartbeats later, deep inside the target.

### Pilgrim of Nowhere

**Role:** Hit-and-fade skirmisher — impossible mobility · **Advances from:** Breathbinder

*Some Breathbinders notice that the road is a suggestion. Pilgrims of Nowhere walk paths that appear on no map and close behind them, arriving before the messenger who announced them. Border lords post rewards; the desperate leave rice at crossroads. Neither has any measurable effect on where a Pilgrim goes.*

**Playstyle.** Never be where the answer lands; step through walls of spears, strike from the flank that did not exist, and leave.

**The road to Pilgrim of Nowhere:**

1. Reach level 25 and walk from the cloister to the sea in three days, though the road takes seven.
2. Cross a sealed border without touching gate, wall, or river, and bring back something only the far side grows.
3. Stand at a crossroads at midnight until you can no longer say which road brought you there, then choose none of them.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Step Between** — Cross several paces instantly, passing through obstacles, foes, and closed doors as though the world briefly forgot them.
- **Crossroads Memory** — Mark a place you have stood; once per battle, return to it from anywhere in sight.
- **Arrive Unannounced** — Your first strike after a step lands as a critical against any foe that has not yet seen you.

### Hollow Hand

**Role:** Spirit-striker — bypasses flesh and armor · **Advances from:** Breathbinder

*The deepest breathing teaches that flesh is a curtain, and a curtain can be reached past. Hollow Hands leave no bruise and stop hearts; they quiet possessing spirits and lay restless dead with a touch. Clergy request them in unsigned letters. No one who has watched them work requests them twice lightly.*

**Playstyle.** Slip past every defense the enemy built and strike the occupant directly; armor, wards, and even flesh are someone else's problem.

**The road to Hollow Hand:**

1. Reach level 25 and snuff a candle from a full pace away without disturbing its smoke.
2. Strike the demonstration bell so it sounds inside each listener's chest and not at all in the air.
3. Lay a restless ghost with one touch, and take nothing from the grieving but your leave.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Curtain Strike** — Your palm passes force through armor, shield, and skin untouched, wounding only what lives underneath.
- **Quieting Touch** — Press two fingers to a caster's sternum; their voice and their magic go silent for a time.
- **Strike the Tenant** — Deal grievous harm to spirits, possessors, and the undead by hitting the ghost rather than the housing.

## Tier IV — Elite (Level 40)

### Stillgate

**Role:** Living fortification — absolute ground control · **Advances from:** Unmoved Pillar

*There are never more than a dozen Stillgates alive, and the war-colleges of three empires chart their whereabouts like weather. When a Stillgate accepts a post, the post holds; that is the order's whole doctrine, and no siege in written memory has amended it. Armies reroute rather than test the record.*

**Playstyle.** Become the terrain; while you hold your ground nothing crosses, nothing moves you, and every plan that assumed otherwise dies expensively.

**The road to Stillgate:**

1. Reach level 40 and hold a breach alone against a named army until relief arrives, or does not.
2. Be offered honorable retreat by heralds three times in one night, and decline three times, courteously.
3. Learn that enemy maps now ink your standing-place as a fortification, and let this change nothing in you.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Post Is Held** — While you remain at your chosen ground, gain immense damage reduction and force all foes in reach to face you.
- **Threshold Law** — Once each round, an enemy attempting to pass you is simply stopped, its momentum returned as staggering force.
- **Doctrine of Remaining** — Once per battle, an attack that would fell you leaves you at one breath instead, provided you have not moved.

### Palm of Ruin

**Role:** Siegebreaker — single blows that end architecture · **Advances from:** Idolbreaker

*Cities have paid the cloister handsomely for the assurance that no Palm of Ruin is currently traveling toward them. These few study a fortress the way physicians study a dying man, then conclude it with one strike. They demolish tyrannies by removing the walls tyranny requires, and they charge nothing for it.*

**Playstyle.** Spend long moments reading, then release a single strike that settles the question; everything about you is preparation for one instant.

**The road to Palm of Ruin:**

1. Reach level 40 and bring down a warded citadel gate with a single strike, after nine days of silent study.
2. Accept a tyrant's payment to raze a village, return the coin, and perform the demolition on his hall instead.
3. Leave one wall of the razed fortress standing, so the survivors have shade to grieve in.

**Abilities granted:**

- **One-Palm Demolition** — After a full round of stillness, deliver a blow that collapses gates, walls, and constructs outright.
- **Ruin Reads the Wall** — Perceive the fault-lines in any structure or armored foe at a glance, through stone if need be.
- **Aftershock Sutra** — Your great strikes echo; the target and everything touching it take a second, lesser toll one heartbeat later.

### Roadless Saint

**Role:** Phantom skirmisher — battlefield-wide presence · **Advances from:** Pilgrim of Nowhere

*A Roadless Saint no longer finds the hidden roads; the roads find them. Witnesses swear one stood in two doorways in the same breath, and both doorways agree. Generals pay for the rumor of a Saint's favor, since the reality cannot be bought, followed, borrowed, or confirmed until it has already happened.*

**Playstyle.** Treat the whole battlefield as adjacent; appear where you are needed, strike where you are not expected, and be counted twice.

**The road to Roadless Saint:**

1. Reach level 40 and attend your own funeral rites in a distant province, leaving without correcting anyone.
2. Guide a hundred refugees through a mountain pass that the pursuing soldiers swear on their lives does not exist.
3. Walk out of a sealed oubliette, and never mention it, even when asked by the one who sealed it.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Two Doorways** — Briefly stand in two places at once; both may strike, and only one was ever really there.
- **The Unwalked Way** — Open a hidden path a short distance; allies may follow before it closes and forgets itself.
- **Absent Step** — Once per round, an attack that should have hit you finds you were never in that place.

### Heart-Stiller

**Role:** Executioner — clean, woundless single-target death · **Advances from:** Hollow Hand

*Heart-Stillers are named in treaties. Certain wars have ended on the rumor that one had taken an interest; certain kings sleep in rooms without doors because of them. They kill without wound and without appetite, and what is more feared, they sometimes decline, and no offer improves the answer.*

**Playstyle.** Choose the one death that ends the battle and administer it precisely; everything else on the field is a distraction to be endured.

**The road to Heart-Stiller:**

1. Reach level 40 and stop a bull's heart with two fingers, restart it with two more, and master the trembling afterward.
2. Be listed by name in a peace treaty as a weapon, and attend the signing unarmed and unintroduced.
3. Refuse a king's execution order to his face, and let the silence escort you out.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Stilling Palm** — Against a weakened foe, stop the heart outright; the body shows no wound and no violence.
- **Held Verdict** — Touch a foe and defer the blow; the stored death lands whenever you speak the word, hours later if you wish.
- **Unstrung Knees** — Strike the resolve rather than the body; enemies around you falter, slow, and lose the will to press.

## Tier V — Mythic (Level 60)

### Seated Mountain

**Role:** Immovable colossus — battlefield-defining bulwark · **Advances from:** Stillgate

*The histories admit to three. A Seated Mountain sits down and becomes geography: empires reroute roads, storms divide, and sieges starve around a figure that has stopped distinguishing itself from the hill it occupies. Pilgrims cannot always tell which weathered shape on the ridge is the one still breathing.*

**Playstyle.** Sit where the world will break and refuse to let it; you are less a combatant than a fixed fact enemies must survive.

**The road to Seated Mountain:**

1. Reach level 60 and seat yourself where the coming war will be worst, before anyone else knows a war is coming.
2. Outlast a siege in which you are the objective; let the army starve around your stillness and go home.
3. Refuse a god's direct command to move, and be found still seated when the shrine is rebuilt around you.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Geography** — While seated, allies behind you cannot be targeted, and weather, spells, and charges divide around your stillness.
- **The Refusal** — Once per battle, decline a catastrophe; one cataclysmic attack, collapse, or working simply does not occur within your reach.
- **Erosion's Pace** — All damage you take is deferred and drained away slowly, as weathering, over hours you can afford.

### Iron Knell

**Role:** Cataclysmic striker — one blow, one ending · **Advances from:** Palm of Ruin

*The falling temple bell, made flesh. When an Iron Knell strikes with intent, the sound arrives in neighboring provinces as a single toll with no bell to blame. Their blows are recorded in the annals as dates, and what stood before each date is described in the past tense.*

**Playstyle.** Everything is wind-up for the one strike; when it lands, the battle reorganizes itself around what is suddenly no longer there.

**The road to Iron Knell:**

1. Reach level 60 and strike the cracked great bell of a fallen temple so it rings true once more, then never again.
2. Unmake with one blow a fortress that took a generation to raise, and stay a season to bury its dead.
3. Let the historians ask what engine did it, and let no answer survive you.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Toll** — Your strike sounds through everything nearby; all who hear it are staggered, deafened, and briefly unable to act.
- **One Date in the Annals** — Once per day, deliver a blow that permanently sunders what it hits; what breaks does not mend.
- **Resonant Marrow** — Your bones ring under impact; whoever strikes you takes the echo of their own blow returned.
- **The Silence After** — For a breath after your great strike, nothing within earshot may move, cast, or cry out.

### Tenth Step

**Role:** Omnipresent phantom — arrives before the need · **Advances from:** Roadless Saint

*Doctrine holds that nine steps cross the world and a tenth leaves it. Those who take the tenth return rarely and out of order, arriving in some accounts before their own departures. Crossroads shrines address them directly. It is considered unwise to be certain a Tenth Step is absent.*

**Playstyle.** Be everywhere the fight needs you, occasionally before it needs you; distance, walls, and sequence have stopped applying to your movement.

**The road to Tenth Step:**

1. Reach level 60 and take the step your doctrine forbids, and tell no one what waited on the far side.
2. Arrive before your own departure, and greet yourself without flinching or asking questions.
3. Hear yourself prayed to at a midnight crossroads, and answer exactly once.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Nine Steps** — Cross any battlefield in nine strides, ignoring walls, wards, chasms, and everything else the world put in the way.
- **The Tenth** — Step outside the world entirely for a breath; return anywhere you can imagine, untouchable while gone.
- **Already There** — Once per battle, be retroactively where an ally needed you; the blow that struck them struck you instead, and you were ready.

### Empty Heaven

**Role:** Transcendent executioner — ends what cannot die · **Advances from:** Heart-Stiller

*The last teaching of the inner path is administered by no one: climb the final stair, strike at heaven, and stand in whatever answers. Those it leaves standing walk back down owning nothing, needing nothing, and able to touch anything — gods included, who are lately difficult to find at home.*

**Playstyle.** Nothing is beyond arm's reach anymore; fight spirits, gods, and abstractions with the same open hand, and end fights by ending the will behind them.

**The road to Empty Heaven:**

1. Reach level 60, climb the mountain's last stair alone, strike once at heaven, and stand in the silence that follows.
2. Still the spirit of something that has never died, and keep its name to yourself forever.
3. Give away your final possession, the self, and walk back down the stairs anyway.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Knocking at Heaven** — Your strikes land on anything with a spirit — ghosts, gods, possessions, curses — regardless of form, ward, or immortality.
- **Nothing to Take** — Owning nothing, you cannot be disarmed, cursed, possessed, or beguiled; hostile magic finds no purchase and slides off.
- **The Hollow Answer** — Still every spirit in reach; summons unravel, blessings fall mute, and the possessed wake as themselves.
- **Unclenched Fist** — End a battle without a death by striking the will to fight from every foe who can still yield.
