# 🕯️ Cleric

> *A mortal lens for a divine and terrible light.*

**Archetype:** Spirit

A Cleric is what happens when a god needs hands. Sworn at a shrine, trained in scripture and field surgery, they carry a mace in one fist and a prayer in the other, learning that faith under pressure is a muscle, not a mood. Some become the god's wrath, some its open channel — and every miracle is entered in a ledger somebody must eventually pay.

## The Tree

```text
Cleric (Lv 1)
├── Warpriest (Lv 10)
│   ├── Judicar (Lv 25) → Absolver (Lv 40) → Ashen Saint (Lv 60)
│   └── Consecrant (Lv 25) → Hierophant of the Breach (Lv 40) → Living Reliquary (Lv 60)
└── Vessel (Lv 10)
    ├── Lazarene (Lv 25) → Threshold Warden (Lv 40) → Wellspring Incarnate (Lv 60)
    └── Godspeaker (Lv 25) → Anathemist (Lv 40) → The Final Word (Lv 60)
```

## Tier I — Initiate (Level 1)

### Cleric

**Role:** Hybrid support — mending and radiant reprisal

*Every shrine keeps a ledger of novices: farm children who prayed and were answered, soldiers who bargained in a ditch and lived. The Cleric is the one who came back to pay. They learn scripture, surgery, and the weight of a mace, because a god's mercy travels through hands that work.*

**Playstyle.** A midline hybrid: close wounds with short litanies, crack the profane with a blessed mace, and bank Favor from both to fuel the next answered prayer.

**Taking the first step:**

1. Keep the vigil-night in your god's shrine, dusk bell to dawn bell, awake and silent; if the candle gutters, begin again.
2. Learn the Eighty Small Prayers by rote, and the field-surgeon's knots besides — the god mends through hands that know their work.
3. Work your first answered prayer before witnesses — a fever broken, a wound closed — and have it entered in the shrine's ledger.
4. Carry a stranger's corpse through unquiet country to consecrated burial, and do not set it down.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Litany of Binding** — Chant a closing verse over an ally's wound; it restores health and stanches bleeding for as long as the verse holds.
- **Censured Blow** — Strike with a consecrated mace; against undead and the oath-broken, the impact flares for additional radiant damage.
- **Vigil Ward** — Raise a brief ward over an ally within reach, absorbing the next blow meant for them.
- **Kindled Favor** — Mending the faithful or striking the profane builds Favor; spend it to double the potency of your next prayer.

## Tier II — Adept (Level 10)

### Warpriest

**Role:** Frontline smiter — melee pressure and battle-blessing · **Advances from:** Cleric

*Some clerics learn the god answers loudest from inside a shield-wall. The Warpriest carries doctrine at mace-length: chrism on the haft, verses timed to the push of pike. Companies pay well for one and sleep better besides, for a Warpriest's blessing is applied like medicine — directly, and without asking.*

**Playstyle.** Fight in the press. Anoint your weapon, bless comrades by the shoulder-clap, and convert every landed blow into wrath the whole line can spend.

**The road to Warpriest:**

1. Reach level 10, then stand your first shield-wall from horn to rout without letting the prayer break in your mouth.
2. Take up the mace of a fallen warpriest and carry it home to their shrine for reconsecration; earn the right to keep it.
3. Bless a company on the eve of battle, and be there, still standing, to bless what remains of it after.
4. Shatter something unholy with your own arms — an idol, a false relic, a fane — and bring a fragment back as proof.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Anointed Haft** — Dress your mace with war-chrism; strikes deal radiant damage and mark the struck for your allies' next blows.
- **Shoulder-Clap Blessing** — Grip a comrade mid-fight to grant vigor and immunity to fear for six heartbeats.
- **Wrathful Doxology** — Roar a verse of judgment; enemies in earshot stagger while the faithful strike harder.
- **Penitent's Toll** — An overhead blow that rings like a struck bell, dazing the target and dragging its fury onto you.

### Vessel

**Role:** Backline conduit — healing and foresight · **Advances from:** Cleric

*Most clerics ask; a Vessel opens. They stop shaping prayers and start hosting answers — mendings no surgeon can chart, dreams that arrive true, sentences in their mouths that belong to someone vast. The temples honor them carefully, the way one honors a door that does not fully close.*

**Playstyle.** A fragile backline channel. Position first, then choose: pour the god into a dying ally, or into three seconds of foresight the enemy cannot afford.

**The road to Vessel:**

1. Reach level 10 and fast in the sanctuary until the first true vision arrives; write it down before you understand it.
2. Heal a wound no surgeon would touch, and accept without complaint the ache it leaves in your own body.
3. Deliver an unwelcome prophecy to someone powerful, word for word, softening nothing.
4. Let the god speak through you once at full voice, and be recognizably yourself the morning after.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Open Channel** — Still yourself completely; your next miracle costs no Favor but takes double from your body instead.
- **Mending Past Craft** — Unmake an injury as though it never landed, restoring health beyond what surgery could explain.
- **Three Seconds Hence** — Glimpse the immediate future; one ally gains the dodge or parry you witnessed.
- **Borrowed Tongue** — Speak a sentence not your own; enemies who hear it falter, having learned something true about their deaths.

## Tier III — Specialist (Level 25)

### Judicar

**Role:** Melee striker — single-target holy execution · **Advances from:** Warpriest

*The crusade needs judges more than it admits. A Judicar reads the charges aloud before the blow falls — apostate priests, false saints, commanders who consecrated what they should have burned. They are licensed to sentence the anointed, which is why bishops greet them warmly and count the silver afterward.*

**Playstyle.** Build a case in combat: name crimes to stack the judgment mark, deny escape and succor, then deliver a verdict that scales with everything proven.

**The road to Judicar:**

1. Reach level 25 and sit the Assize of Embers: recite the crimes of ten heresiarchs from memory, and the lawful sentence for each.
2. Carry out a sealed sentence against one of your own order, alone, and return the seal unbroken.
3. Refuse a bribe worth more than your life, and enter the offer, with names, into the record.
4. Hear the full confession of the one you were sent to kill, and proceed unless the god stays your hand.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Read the Charges** — Name the target's offenses aloud; each charge deepens their judgment mark and amplifies your eventual verdict.
- **Verdict** — A two-handed sentence of a blow; damage rises with accumulated charges and the target's missing health.
- **No Sanctuary** — While your eyes hold them, the marked cannot be healed, hidden, or spirited away.
- **Iron Contrition** — Executing a marked sinner remits your own wounds, restoring health for every charge proven.

### Consecrant

**Role:** Frontline support — consecrated ground and warband blessing · **Advances from:** Warpriest

*Where a Consecrant walks, the earth is sworn in. They hallow trenches into naves, turn a shield-wall's footing into church floor, and plant standards the dead cannot pass. Siege captains requisition them like ordnance, and old soldiers polish their boots before crossing ground a Consecrant has claimed.*

**Playstyle.** Claim ground and make it argue for you: plant standards, chain blessings down the line, and let trespassers ring the unseen bell.

**The road to Consecrant:**

1. Reach level 25 and reconsecrate a ruined chapel alone, threshold-stone to roof-beam, over forty days of unbroken rite.
2. Hold one piece of hallowed ground against desecration until relieved, however long relief takes to come.
3. Bury the dead of both armies after a battle, and hallow the field so thoroughly that neither side rises.
4. Learn the boundary-rites of three orders not your own, well enough to say where each of them errs.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Hallowing Stride** — Ground you cross stays consecrated for a time; allies standing on it resist fear and bleed slower.
- **Planted Standard** — Drive a blessed standard into the earth; nearby allies gain armor and the dead cannot rise in its shadow.
- **Rite of the Unbroken Line** — Chain a blessing through adjacent allies; while the line holds, damage is shared and none can be routed.
- **Trespass Bell** — Unhallowed creatures entering your ground ring a bell only you hear, taking radiant damage and standing revealed.

### Lazarene

**Role:** Dedicated healer — triage, transference, and revival · **Advances from:** Vessel

*Named for the first one raised, Lazarenes work where medicine surrenders: plague-houses, drowned villages, the third day after a battle. Their miracles keep a ledger — every returned breath is owed, and they know precisely what a life costs. It makes them gentle, exact, and difficult to sit beside at supper.*

**Playstyle.** A ledger-keeping healer. Stay deaths rather than prevent all damage, take wounds into your own flesh, and spend banked breath on saves medicine cannot copy.

**The road to Lazarene:**

1. Reach level 25 and serve a full plague-season in a lazar-house, leaving only when the last bell stops ringing.
2. Bring back someone gone past all argument, and pay what it costs without haggling.
3. Sit deathwatch for a stranger from first rattle to last stillness, and learn what dying asks of the living.
4. Keep your ledger honest: record every life you lost as carefully as every life you kept.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Ledger of Breath** — Every life you save is entered; spend entries to fuel miracles beyond medicine's reach.
- **Stay the Hour** — An ally struck down does not die for ten heartbeats; what you do with them is yours.
- **Take the Wound** — Draw an ally's injury into your own flesh at half its weight; both of you keep the scar.
- **Second Breath** — Call a soul back across the threshold within a candle's burning; it returns owing, and so do you.

### Godspeaker

**Role:** Control support — foresight and spoken fate · **Advances from:** Vessel

*The visions come whether or not a Godspeaker asks. Kings keep them close and never comfortable; generals hear how battles end before the first arrow and hate them for it. The god does not soften anything, and neither, by vow, may they — clarity is the whole office.*

**Playstyle.** Fight from prophecy: call enemy actions to cancel them, hand allies the first strike against what you foresaw, and pay the toll for every silence.

**The road to Godspeaker:**

1. Reach level 25 and endure the Long Seeing: nine days blindfolded in the crypt while the visions run their course.
2. Prophesy in public, be laughed at, and say nothing at all when it comes true.
3. Carry a doom to a village that cannot be saved, and stay to help them meet it.
4. Refuse, once, to look — and learn what the god does with a closed eye.

**Abilities granted:**

- **It Was Written** — Declare an enemy's next action aloud; if it comes to pass, it fails, having been foretold.
- **Thread of Hours** — Glimpse the fight's coming moments; allies you warn strike first against what you saw.
- **Unwelcome Truth** — Tell an enemy how it dies; it fights the rest of the battle knowing.
- **Prophet's Toll** — Each prophecy spoken this battle sharpens the next; each withheld bleeds you slowly.

## Tier IV — Elite (Level 40)

### Absolver

**Role:** Elite executioner — burst elimination of the condemned · **Advances from:** Judicar

*When excommunication fails, the church sends its last argument. Absolvers hold that a clean death under the rite cleanses what a whole life could not, and they deliver it personally. Few are named, fewer seen twice; towns remember their visits the way coastlines remember storms — by what is missing.*

**Playstyle.** Hunt one target through anything. The rite makes you unstoppable while a sentence stands open; finish it clean, carry the sins, move on.

**The road to Absolver:**

1. Reach level 40, then answer a summons that arrives with no sender, and survive the year of testing it begins.
2. Execute a sentence you privately believe is wrong, or refuse it before the conclave; the order keeps both kinds of scar.
3. Grant the full rite to someone who wronged you personally, and mean every word of it.
4. Surrender your birth name and enter the rolls under the name of your first condemned.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Rite of Absolution** — Open the rite over a condemned target; until it closes, you cannot be slowed, silenced, or turned aside.
- **The Clean Death** — A sanctified killing blow that cannot be survived below a quarter health; the slain cannot rise or be raised.
- **Bear the Weight** — Carry the target's unabsolved sins as temporary strength; carry too many at once and they begin to whisper.
- **Sealed Verdict** — Nothing enters or leaves a small circle around you and the condemned until one of you is finished.

### Hierophant of the Breach

**Role:** Anchor support — unbreakable zone control · **Advances from:** Consecrant

*When a wall fails, the order sends a wall. Hierophants of the Breach stand where masonry died and make the gap holier than the gate was; siege commanders request them like ordnance and record them like weather. In the annals, every breach they held is rewritten as a gate.*

**Playstyle.** Plant yourself at the worst chokepoint and deepen. The longer you hold, the harder your ground argues; movement is a last resort, almost a heresy.

**The road to Hierophant of the Breach:**

1. Reach level 40 and hold a breach through a full assault, until the enemy sounds withdrawal or the wall is remade behind you.
2. Complete the forty-verse hallowing under bombardment without dropping a syllable.
3. Refuse a lawful order to retreat, and be proven right by dawn.
4. Carry a broken company's standard back through the breach where it died, and hallow the ground where you plant it.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Ground Remembers** — Your consecration deepens each minute held, stacking armor, warding, and dread for anything unhallowed that trespasses.
- **Gate That Held** — Declare a breach a gate; allies passing through it mend, enemies passing through it burn.
- **Unceasing Vigil** — While you have not moved this battle, your blessings cost nothing and cannot be dispelled.
- **Censer Wall** — Swing the great censer in a slow arc; its smoke hangs as a wall the profane cannot cross.

### Threshold Warden

**Role:** Elite healer — death denial and soul retrieval · **Advances from:** Lazarene

*They stand at the door the dying use and negotiate there, lawfully, in person. A Warden can hold the door shut against a massacre or walk through it to fetch someone back — but they may also shake their head, and no verdict in the world is quieter or more final.*

**Playstyle.** Anchor the edge of a fight and intercept deaths as they happen. Your exhaustion is the true resource; refusal is a weapon you rarely draw.

**The road to Threshold Warden:**

1. Reach level 40, then die once, briefly, under the order's supervision, and return with the door's location fixed in memory.
2. Refuse to bring back a soul the whole world wanted returned, and carry the grieving's anger without defending yourself.
3. Sit the threshold through a massacre's aftermath, sorting the crossing from the called-back, until every soul is accounted.
4. Learn the far side of the door well enough to find your way blind, and never once speak of it.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Hold the Door** — No ally in your sight dies while you keep your feet; every prevented death exhausts you further.
- **Walk the Soul Back** — Cross after a soul dead less than a day and lead it home, paying the toll yourself.
- **Denied the Door** — Deny an enemy the door; it cannot die, and carries its wounds screaming until you release it.
- **Quiet Crossing** — Grant a peaceful crossing to the suffering; against the undying, this counts as destruction.

### Anathemist

**Role:** Dread controller — binding curses and pronounced dooms · **Advances from:** Godspeaker

*A Godspeaker repeats what the god says; an Anathemist is trusted with the god's no. Their pronouncements bind — crops, bloodlines, city gates. Courts fall silent when one stands, because an anathema, once spoken, cannot be argued with, only served. Most write their dooms rather than risk saying them.*

**Playstyle.** Weaponized prophecy: lay dooms that ripen over the fight, silence what the god has cursed, and make every enemy plan cost more than it wins.

**The road to Anathemist:**

1. Reach level 40 and copy the Register of Dooms by hand, error-free, learning what each pronouncement did to its speaker.
2. Pronounce your first anathema before the conclave, and stand present, years later, when it completes.
3. Lift a curse an elder of your order laid wrongly, and survive both the curse and the elder.
4. Hold an anathema unspoken on the tip of your tongue for a year, and let it die there unsaid.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Anathema** — Pronounce a doom upon one enemy; it ripens each round until the fated blow lands twofold.
- **Interdiction** — Bar a place or object by decree; enemies who use it anyway are marked and weakened.
- **The God's No** — Countermand one enemy spell or miracle as it happens; its wielder is struck mute for a breath.
- **Written Doom** — Ink a sentence and seal it; whoever breaks the seal inherits the doom inside.

## Tier V — Mythic (Level 60)

### Ashen Saint

**Role:** Mythic striker — final judgment made flesh · **Advances from:** Absolver

*Each generation is granted at most one. The Ashen Saint has been through the god's fire and come out with nothing left that burns: no fear, no favor, no appetite for mercy beyond the exact amount owed. The church canonizes them while they live — an honor invented to keep them supervised.*

**Playstyle.** Walk, judge, conclude. You are slow, inevitable, and untouchable by anything with sin on it; the daily sentence decides battles before they start.

**The road to Ashen Saint:**

1. Reach level 60, walk unarmed into a place your church refuses to name, and judge what rules there.
2. Burn on a heretic's pyre and step down from it when the argument is settled.
3. Accept canonization while still breathing, over the objections of everyone who loves you.
4. Judge yourself last, aloud and in public, and carry out whatever sentence the god sets.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Judgment Entire** — Pronounce final judgment on one creature each day; no armor, oath, or rival god's favor mitigates the sentence.
- **Pyre Unlit** — The unrepentant near you smolder without flame, and lies spoken in your presence blister the tongue.
- **Canon of Ash** — Where your sentence lands, the ash consecrates the ground and every witness's oath becomes binding.
- **Sainted Flesh** — Blows against you fall away as ash; only doubt can cut you, and yours burned long ago.

### Living Reliquary

**Role:** Mythic anchor — walking sanctuary and mass ward · **Advances from:** Hierophant of the Breach

*They stop carrying holy things and become the holy thing carried. Pilgrim roads bend toward wherever a Living Reliquary is standing; shrines assemble around their stillness without masons; seven cathedrals already hold deeds to their bones. Armies have surrendered rather than fight across ground that walks.*

**Playstyle.** You are the sacred ground. Every ally in your shadow fights under sanctuary law; move rarely, deliberately, and let the battlefield reorganize around you.

**The road to Living Reliquary:**

1. Reach level 60 and accept the Enshrinement: a relic sealed into your living flesh, whose demands you must survive.
2. Hallow ground openly in the enemy's capital, and leave by the front gate.
3. Let pilgrims wear a path to your door and turn none away, whatever they carry.
4. Watch the shrine they build around your stillness outgrow your name, and stay anyway.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Ambulatory Sanctum** — You are consecrated ground; every rule of sanctuary applies within your shadow, wherever it falls.
- **Pilgrim's Touch** — Allies who lay a hand on you shed curses, fear, and one mortal wound per day.
- **The Promised Bones** — You cannot be destroyed on hallowed ground, and you are always on hallowed ground.
- **Bell Beneath the Skin** — When you are struck, a bell tolls across the field; the faithful rally and the profane falter.

### Wellspring Incarnate

**Role:** Mythic healer — mass restoration and return · **Advances from:** Threshold Warden

*The lens stops channeling the light and becomes it. A Wellspring Incarnate no longer prays for mercy; they seep it — plagues end in provinces they cross, and a battlefield's dead can rise breathing on a single exhalation. Histories argue whether each was one person or several; the wells named after them still heal.*

**Playstyle.** Radiance without effort: everything near you mends, always. The discipline is choosing what not to heal, and how much of yourself each great miracle spends.

**The road to Wellspring Incarnate:**

1. Reach level 60, then empty yourself into one miracle too large to survive, and be found alive anyway, three days on.
2. Close your ledger: forgive every debt of breath owed to you, by name, aloud.
3. End a plague by walking its province end to end, sleeping only in the sick-houses.
4. Let the god pour through until you cannot tell which memories are yours, and keep the ones that matter anyway.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Overflowing** — You heal everything near you, constantly; choosing who not to heal is now the discipline.
- **The Great Remission** — Once a season, undo a catastrophe's wounds across a mile; sleep a week for every hundred restored.
- **One Exhalation** — Rekindle the newly dead across the field in a single breath; each returns with your heartbeat in their chest.
- **Springhead** — Wherever you finally die, a healing spring will rise; you have not yet tested this.

### The Final Word

**Role:** Mythic controller — fate pronounced and enforced · **Advances from:** Anathemist

*At the end of prophecy, speech stops predicting and starts deciding. The Final Word talks rarely — some not for years — because everything said comes due. Treaties hold if one merely attended the signing. Chroniclers record their sentences the way they record eclipses: exactly, and from a distance.*

**Playstyle.** An economy of absolute utterances. Hold your silence like a drawn weapon, position for the perfect sentence, and spend words the way others spend lives.

**The road to The Final Word:**

1. Reach level 60 and keep a decade's vow of silence, breaking it once, for a sentence the histories still argue over.
2. Speak your own ending aloud — date, place, and manner — and live toward it without flinching.
3. Refuse the god a word once: keep your mouth shut when it wants opening, and bear the cost.
4. Attend a war's last parley, end it with one sentence, and walk home unescorted.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Pronouncement** — Speak a fate aloud once per battle; reality complies within the hour by the shortest road it finds.
- **The Kept Silence** — While you have not spoken, allies act as though forewarned and enemies as though accused.
- **Unwriting** — Declare one recent event untrue; the world keeps the scar but not the wound.
- **Witnessed** — Anything sworn in your hearing binds; oathbreakers learn what your attention costs.
