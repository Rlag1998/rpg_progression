# 🐲 Sorcerer

> *The blood decides; the sorcerer merely aims.*

**Archetype:** Arcane

No tutor chose them and no book explains them. Sorcery arrived with the blood — in a fever, a struck bell, a house fire nobody could account for. Every Sorcerer spends their first years surviving their own gift: grounding it, starving it, apologizing for it. The great ones stop apologizing. They learn that a flaw, aimed well, is indistinguishable from a weapon.

## The Tree

```text
Sorcerer (Lv 1)
├── Wyrmblooded (Lv 10)
│   ├── Cinderthroat (Lv 25) → Voice of the Furnace (Lv 40) → Worldpyre (Lv 60)
│   └── Scalewrought (Lv 25) → The Molted (Lv 40) → The Ancestor (Lv 60)
└── Stormveined (Lv 10)
    ├── Levinbrand (Lv 25) → Eye of the Tempest (Lv 40) → Standing Storm (Lv 60)
    └── Chancewright (Lv 25) → Hazard Saint (Lv 40) → The Unfated (Lv 60)
```

## Tier I — Initiate (Level 1)

### Sorcerer

**Role:** Ranged caster — volatile raw burst

*Every village has a story: the child who wept sparks, the granary that burned on naming-day. Sorcerers begin as accidents that lived. Hedge-wives dose them, priests test them, war-camps eventually buy them. Most spend their first years learning not to kill anyone by accident, and the rest of their lives learning to do it on purpose.*

**Playstyle.** Volatile ranged caster. Spend raw power fast, ride surges as they come, and manage a gift that grows stronger the closer it drifts toward slipping.

**Taking the first step:**

1. Survive the night your gift first wakes — the burned bed, the frightened kin, the silence after.
2. Find one person who does not flinch from you, and learn to cast with them in the room.
3. Ground a surge into cold earth without fainting, three times, witnessed by no one who might use you.
4. Name your power aloud. Not curse, not blessing — yours.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Bloodspark** — Hurl a bolt of raw sorcery drawn straight from the vein; damage rises with every surge suffered this battle.
- **Gutter the Flame** — Clamp your gift shut for one breath, purging a curse or magical binding from yourself as the power dies down.
- **Grounding Rite** — Kneel and bleed excess power into earth or iron, trading your next action for stability.
- **Surge Fault** — Passive — your spells sometimes echo a heartbeat later at half strength, whether or not you wanted them to.

## Tier II — Adept (Level 10)

### Wyrmblooded

**Role:** Midrange caster — sustained fire pressure · **Advances from:** Sorcerer

*The dreams come first — one mountain, always seen from above. Then heat in the spit, gold weighed too long in the palm, the first scales at the nape. Wyrmblooded sorcerers are courted by armies and watched carefully by everyone else, because whatever ancestor stirs in them was never entirely human, and it is not finished waking.*

**Playstyle.** Sustained fire pressure from midrange. Build heat with every cast, wear it as armor, and grow harder to kill the longer the fight burns.

**The road to Wyrmblooded:**

1. Reach level 10 and dream the same mountain three nights running, then wake with heat in your mouth.
2. Trace the old rumor in your family line to a grave, a hoard, or a lie — and forgive none of it.
3. Let the first scales come. Do not cut them out.
4. Breathe fire once, deliberately, at something you chose to burn.

**Abilities granted:**

- **First Ember** — Exhale a gout of ancestral fire; targets left burning take deepened damage from your next spell.
- **Underscale** — Scales surface beneath the skin when you are struck, granting stacking armor that lasts while your heat holds.
- **Ancestor's Pulse** — Once per battle the older heart beats for yours, refusing a killing blow and igniting you fully.
- **Hoard-Sense** — Passive — you feel gold, gems, and hidden bodies through stone, the way the ancestor once did.

### Stormveined

**Role:** Ranged caster — high-variance burst · **Advances from:** Sorcerer

*Their moods read on barometers. Stormveined sorcerers crackle — hair lifting before they anger, candle flames leaning toward them, dice behaving strangely at their tables. Captains want them in the charge and keep them far from the powder stores. Their power is not disciplined; it is negotiated with, hourly, and it respects nothing but nerve.*

**Playstyle.** High-variance burst caster. Court wild surges instead of suppressing them, cash lucky outcomes into damage, and gamble position on lightning-fast repositioning.

**The road to Stormveined:**

1. Reach level 10 and stand in open weather through a storm you did not summon, until it notices you.
2. Lose control once in public. Pay for it. Remember the exact color of the moment before.
3. Wager something irreplaceable on a coin toss and let the magic call it in the air.
4. Learn the smell of a surge coming — copper and rain — and stop flinching.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Levin Snap** — Loose a jolting arc that rolls its damage twice and keeps the higher, then jumps to a second target.
- **Sideways Luck** — Passive — each wild surge you suffer banks a charm; spend charms to reroll any outcome near you once.
- **Hackle Field** — Static sheathes you; melee attackers are jolted, slowed, and briefly certain they smelled rain.
- **Ride the Fault** — Break apart into a crack of lightning and reform anywhere in sight, arriving before your thunder does.

## Tier III — Specialist (Level 25)

### Cinderthroat

**Role:** Cone caster — burning area denial · **Advances from:** Wyrmblooded

*In some, the wyrm settles in the lungs. Cinderthroats speak in ruined voices and glow faintly at the collarbone when angry. Siege companies rent them by the gate; villages that hear one coughing evacuate without instruction. They are the reason certain fortresses keep doors of solid stone and generals of unusual politeness.*

**Playstyle.** Positional cone caster. Manage banked breath and scorch stacks, cut battlefields into burning lanes, and hold ground no one can afford to cross.

**The road to Cinderthroat:**

1. Reach level 25 and swallow a live coal from a dragon-touched fire, and keep it down.
2. Hold one breath through a burning building's collapse, then walk out and speak clearly.
3. Reduce a fortified gate to slag while its defenders watch, and refuse the payment offered after.
4. Learn which of your words scorch, and stop wasting them.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Rendering Breath** — A slow, widening cone of furnace-heat that melts armor value away before it ever touches flesh.
- **Second Lung** — Bank one breath of fire; release it instantly later, even while silenced, grappled, or drowning.
- **Cauter Line** — Draw a burning seam across the ground that persists, splitting the field into yours and theirs.
- **Glasswake** — Your fire fuses soil into glass; enemies crossing your scorched ground walk slowly, on knives.

### Scalewrought

**Role:** Frontline battle-caster — attrition tank · **Advances from:** Wyrmblooded

*These are the ones who stopped fighting the molt. Plate spreads over ribs and forearms; gauntlets become unnecessary, then impossible. Scalewrought walk the front rank of battles that expected no caster within reach, shrugging arrows and casting through wounds. What unsettles people is not the scale. It is how calm they have become since accepting it.*

**Playstyle.** Frontline battle-caster. Absorb hits to bank heat, cast at claw's length, and outlast enemies who mistook you for the soft target.

**The road to Scalewrought:**

1. Reach level 25 and endure a full molt awake, keeping count of every scale that comes.
2. Take a blade meant for someone else and let them watch it fail against you.
3. Sleep once, unarmored, among people who fear you, and wake having harmed no one.
4. Stop apologizing for your hands.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Plated Hour** — Scale closes over you entirely for a span; damage taken banks as heat your claws may spend.
- **Wyrmgrip** — Seize a foe in one taloned hand; spells cast into a held enemy cannot miss or be resisted.
- **Molt Burst** — Shed a layer of scale explosively, shredding everything adjacent and tearing every binding off you.
- **Slow Furnace** — Passive — you regenerate while standing in fire. Any fire. Anyone's.

### Levinbrand

**Role:** Mobile skirmisher — chain lightning strikes · **Advances from:** Stormveined

*The Stormveined who chose the lightning over the luck. Levinbrands cross provinces between dusk and dinner and end duels before their thunder arrives. Border forts log their passage as weather rather than visitors. The speed, they will tell you, was always free; it is the standing still that has to be trained.*

**Playstyle.** Hyper-mobile skirmisher-caster. Blink through enemy lines as living lightning, chain arcs off armored targets, and bank charges from everything you evade.

**The road to Levinbrand:**

1. Reach level 25 and outrun your own thunder on open ground, before a sworn skeptic.
2. Take one strike from the sky standing up, and keep whatever it leaves in your bones.
3. Cross an active battlefield touching the ground three times or fewer.
4. Practice stillness one hour a day. The speed is free; the stopping will cost you.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Stepwise Lightning** — Become the bolt along a chosen line, striking everything on it and arriving before your own sound.
- **Chained Verdict** — An arc that leaps from target to target, favoring whoever carries the most metal.
- **Afterimage Charge** — The place you just stood detonates in static one heartbeat after you leave it.
- **Capacitor Heart** — Passive — every attack you evade banks a charge; spend charges to cast instantly, without pause or breath.

### Chancewright

**Role:** Support disruptor — probability control · **Advances from:** Stormveined

*The Stormveined who followed the luck instead of the bolt. Chancewrights are banned from honest card-rooms by portrait and welcomed into war-tents by generals who want the enemy's plans stood next to. Around them, accidents stop being accidents and become negotiations — negotiations the Chancewright has usually opened already.*

**Playstyle.** Probability manipulator and disruptor. Load outcomes for allies, sour them for enemies, and stake your own blood when the odds need forcing.

**The road to Chancewright:**

1. Reach level 25 and win a rigged game without touching the rig — bend only the luck around it.
2. Lose everything once, deliberately, and note exactly where the laws of chance felt strained.
3. Cross an active battlefield unarmed; collect other people's arrows in your coat and none in you.
4. Flip one coin a thousand times and record the day it starts refusing to land.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Loaded Hour** — For a span, every random outcome nearby rolls twice; allies keep the better result, enemies the worse.
- **Fumble Clause** — Curse one certainty — the strap breaks, the footing slips, the sure spell sours.
- **House Edge** — Passive — every enemy miss near you banks a token; spend three to declare one outcome outright.
- **Wager of Flesh** — Stake your own blood on a spell: it casts twice if it lands, costs double if it fails.

## Tier IV — Elite (Level 40)

### Voice of the Furnace

**Role:** Siege caster — field-scale annihilation · **Advances from:** Cinderthroat

*Rare enough that cities negotiate before one arrives. A Voice speaks and mortar runs; their breath is surveyed in acres afterward. Several kingdoms keep registries of the living ones, and at least two pay a standing annual fee for the simple assurance of absence. Their footprints stay warm for days.*

**Playstyle.** Siege-scale annihilation. Long wind-ups into field-erasing exhalations; reshape terrain, devour enemy fire, and make the far half of any battle uninhabitable.

**The road to Voice of the Furnace:**

1. Reach level 40 and unmake a siege alone — the walls, the engines, and the appetite for another.
2. Refuse a king's contract to burn a city, then survive whatever he sends to insist.
3. Write your name into a cliff face with a single word.
4. Find a young Cinderthroat and teach them for nothing. The fire keeps its own accounts.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Sermon of Ash** — A sustained exhalation that widens as it travels, converting the far end of a battlefield to cinders.
- **Firebreak** — Inhale an incoming fire spell whole and keep it, adding its strength to your next breath.
- **Kilnheart** — Your core burns openly; nearby allied weapons take on a working edge of flame.
- **The Long Vowel** — Hold one burning note; while it sounds, no flame on the field obeys anyone but you.

### The Molted

**Role:** Transformed juggernaut — formation breaker · **Advances from:** Scalewrought

*The final human skin comes off whole, and the order keeps them — pale, folded, labeled — in a cold hall under the mountain. What walks out afterward still speaks, still keeps oaths, still remembers birthdays. Veterans would rather face anything else on a wall, because the Molted come on through everything.*

**Playstyle.** Unstoppable frontline engine. Wade through massed fire, convert punishment into strength, and dismantle formations at claw's length while spellwork seals the exits.

**The road to The Molted:**

1. Reach level 40 and shed the last human skin whole, then fold it yourself, without help.
2. Breach a shield-wall using nothing you learned from books.
3. Carry your shed skin to the cold hall beneath the mountain and shelve it beside the others.
4. Keep one human habit on purpose. The order recommends birthdays.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Second Shape** — Assume the between-form fully — talon, tail, and wingframe — trading spell range for irresistible reach.
- **Adamant Molt** — Each battle's first molt leaves a standing husk that fights on beside you for a time.
- **Furnace Blood** — Your blood ignites as it leaves you; whoever wounds you in melee shares the burn.
- **Graven Scale** — Inscribe one oath upon your plate; while you keep it, that scale cannot be pierced.

### Eye of the Tempest

**Role:** Zone controller — mobile storm fortress · **Advances from:** Levinbrand

*Elite enough that admiralties track them like weather systems, because they are one. An Eye walks inside a moving calm perhaps ten paces wide; everything beyond it is rim, and the rim is catastrophe. Ports have surrendered to nothing more than the sight of one standing dry in the harbor rain.*

**Playstyle.** Mobile fortress of weather. Anchor a traveling storm around your own calm, punish every approach, and dictate where the battle is allowed to stand.

**The road to Eye of the Tempest:**

1. Reach level 40 and walk a hurricane across open sea, then park it, whole, where you chose.
2. Winter alone on a peak that lightning loves, and come down carrying its patience.
3. End a battle with no bolt landing — the threat, held steady, sufficing.
4. Refuse the shrine the coast builds you. Insist on being weather, not god.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Standing Vortex** — Anchor a hurricane to your heel; its walls travel with you and its calm belongs to you alone.
- **Barometric Drop** — Collapse the air over a zone — flyers fall, ears burst, and every flame dies at once.
- **Nine Bolts Waiting** — Hold sky-charges aloft; they fall, in order, on the next nine things to cross your rim.
- **Lull** — Silence the entire storm for three heartbeats, so everyone can consider what you are choosing not to do.

### Hazard Saint

**Role:** Fate warper — ally miracles, enemy ruin · **Advances from:** Chancewright

*Mercenary companies carry their tokens; insurers refuse whole districts where one sleeps. They are called saints because prayers to luck get answered in their vicinity — promptly, precisely, and at a price the parish only tallies later. Disasters do not avoid a Hazard Saint's debtors. They reroute.*

**Playstyle.** Field-warping support. Consecrate ground where plans decay, underwrite allies' impossible attempts, and take catastrophes onto yourself to break them into inconveniences.

**The road to Hazard Saint:**

1. Reach level 40 and survive your own execution through a chain of accidents no court can call intent.
2. Settle every debt you owe to chance. A saint borrows for others now.
3. Be prayed to once, in earnest, and answer without letting them see the price.
4. Bless a hopeless charge and walk at the front of it, unarmored.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Canon of Errors** — Consecrate ground where nothing proceeds as planned; enemy orders arrive garbled and reinforcements arrive lost.
- **Miracle Ledger** — Record an ally's impossible attempt; once per day, one recorded entry simply succeeds.
- **Martyr's Odds** — Take a catastrophe aimed elsewhere onto yourself, then shatter it into a hundred harmless inconveniences.
- **Sainted Coin** — Flip: heads, your next spell is free and doubled; tails, your enemy's is. You may not look first.

## Tier V — Mythic (Level 60)

### Worldpyre

**Role:** Apocalyptic artillery — terrain erasure · **Advances from:** Voice of the Furnace

*Histories count them in single digits and disagree about those. Where a Worldpyre fought, the maps simply note glass. Scholars argue their breath is the first fire — the one the eldest wyrms carried before there were things to burn. When a Worldpyre dies, witnesses report, the pyre lights itself.*

**Playstyle.** Apocalyptic zone erasure. Slow, inexorable, terrain-rewriting devastation. Your presence renegotiates where armies are willing to exist, and every engagement becomes a question of evacuation routes.

**The road to Worldpyre:**

1. Reach level 60 and breathe against a true dragon's fire until one of you looks away first.
2. Stand in a caldera as it wakes and breathe the eruption back down its own throat.
3. Spare a city you were paid, provoked, and entitled to burn — and be believed when you explain.
4. Burn the name your family gave the ancestor. Give the fire yours instead.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The First Fire** — Breathe the flame the eldest wyrms remember; it does not spread — it replaces.
- **Glass Meridian** — Draw a horizon-long line of vitrified earth that no army will cross for a generation.
- **Ashfall Covenant** — For one day, everything within sight burns only with your permission.
- **Pyre Eternal** — Passive — should you fall, your last breath lights a fire that dies only when your body reaches home.

### The Ancestor

**Role:** Draconic avatar — battlefield-ending presence · **Advances from:** The Molted

*The molt completes, and the truth arrives with it: the wyrm was never behind them in the bloodline. It was ahead, waiting. What surfaces wears centuries comfortably and answers to no childhood name. Chroniclers dispute whether the sorcerer survives the becoming or merely furnishes it; the Ancestor declines to clarify.*

**Playstyle.** Draconic avatar. Flight, claimed ground, and a presence that ends most fights before they begin — devastating in the few that begin anyway.

**The road to The Ancestor:**

1. Reach level 60 and finish the molt that has no other side.
2. Fly to the mountain from the dreams and find your own claw-marks already old there.
3. Hear your childhood name spoken and feel nothing in you turn toward it.
4. Outlive the last person who knew you human, and grieve them properly.
5. Begin a bloodline. Somewhere far down it, someone will dream a mountain.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Full Surfacing** — The last human proportions give way — wings, span, and a shadow armies recognize from stories.
- **Weight of Ages** — Your presence presses down like accumulated centuries; lesser wills kneel, flee, or empty their hands.
- **Hoardbind** — Claim ground and everything upon it; enemies on your claim move as through deep water.
- **Long Memory** — Wounds dealt to you are remembered against the giver's whole line, and the debt collects interest.

### Standing Storm

**Role:** Storm incarnate — regional devastation · **Advances from:** Eye of the Tempest

*Sailors insist there is weather that thinks, and the almanacs have begun agreeing. A Standing Storm appears twice in good histories — once as a person, once as a recorded meteorological event, and the dates align. Whether a body still walks at the center is a question the rain declines to answer.*

**Playstyle.** Storm incarnate. Disperse into weather and reform at will, govern the skies over whole regions, and fight as a front rather than a figure.

**The road to Standing Storm:**

1. Reach level 60 and let the storm carry you, dissolved, a hundred miles — then reassemble unafraid.
2. Contest a weather-god above its own temple and settle for the draw.
3. Be entered twice in the same history: once as a name, once as a storm, dates matching.
4. Choose, once, over a place that wronged you, to rain gently.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Weatherfront Body** — Disperse into the storm itself; blades part rain, and the rain answers back with lightning.
- **Season of Wrath** — Declare the weather over a region for days; the sky honors your grief or your mercy exactly.
- **The First Thunder** — One peal in which every plan requiring sound, sight, or steady nerve fails simultaneously.
- **Rainfall Census** — Passive — you feel everything your rain touches; nothing moves under your sky unnumbered.

### The Unfated

**Role:** Reality editor — outcome dictation · **Advances from:** Hazard Saint

*Oracles skip them mid-prophecy without noticing the gap. Their pages arrive blank in books that were finished centuries ago. Historians find records of the Unfated erased not by any hand but by probability quietly withdrawing its cooperation. Whether they still choose things, or simply are what happens, remains — fittingly — undetermined.*

**Playstyle.** Outcome dictation. Unhappen events, declare endings in advance, and fight as a target no divination, prediction, or aimed fate can find.

**The road to The Unfated:**

1. Reach level 60 and visit the oracle who has never been wrong. Watch her look through you. Apologize.
2. Unhappen one great grief, and learn precisely why you will never do it again.
3. Keep one thing certain — small, secret, yours — as an anchor, or drift.
4. Stand inside a history as it erases you, and smile at the space.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Torn Page** — Your future cannot be read, targeted, or foretold; divinations cast at you return only weather.
- **The Unhappened** — Once per battle, one event that just occurred, did not.
- **Dictated Ending** — Declare how a duel ends before it begins; the world spends the fight arranging it.
- **Probability Zero** — Step briefly through an outcome no dice could reach — unharmed, elsewhere, already acting.
