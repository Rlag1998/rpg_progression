# 👁️ Warlock

> *Power on credit. The interest compounds nightly.*

**Archetype:** Arcane

Every Warlock owns a signature they cannot take back. Some signed for knowledge, some for revenge, one or two for love; all signed. Power arrives on schedule — the price is deferred, compounding, and collected in currencies no one thinks to guard. A Warlock's art is spending borrowed fire faster than the interest accrues, and reading every word before speaking any.

## Tier I — Initiate (Level 1)

### Warlock

**Role:** Ranged caster — sustained pact damage

*Warlocks are found, not trained. A scholar who read one margin too deep. A farmer who took the fourth road at a three-way crossing. Each carries a pact instead of a pedigree, and power that answers promptly — because prompt service is how a lender keeps a client borrowing. Guilds mistrust them. Lenders never do.*

**Playstyle.** Sustained pact-bolts fueled by a debt meter that recharges when you take risks; spend health, position, or buffs as collateral for bursts of borrowed power.

**Taking the first step:**

1. Want something more than you fear the price — enough that the wanting can be heard.
2. Find the offer: a book that answers back, a crossroads with a fourth road, a dream that leaves ink on your hands.
3. Sign. With blood, with a name, with the year you would have died happy — but sign.
4. Cast your first borrowed spell, and feel the interest begin.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Umbral Levy** — Fires a bolt of borrowed shadow that deals sustained damage and refunds a sliver of your debt meter on kill.
- **Witness Mark** — Brands a target as observed by your patron, increasing your damage against it and revealing it through walls.
- **Borrowed Fire** — Spend health as collateral to double your next spell's damage; the health returns only if the spell kills.
- **Read the Terms** — Study an enemy for one turn to learn a resistance and gain a shield scaled to what you learned.

## Tier II — Adept (Level 10)

### Brimstone Advocate

**Role:** Mid-range controller — burning debuffs and bindings · **Advances from:** Warlock

*Advocates argue with hell in hell's own grammar. They study clause-law in chapterhouses whose libraries smoke faintly, and treat their pact as a live case rather than a settled debt. Devils find them insufferable and, worse, admissible. Their spellbook reads like a brief, and their fires arrive with citations attached.*

**Playstyle.** Mid-range control through burning contract sigils; brand enemies with terms, punish them for breaching, and cash accumulated clauses into sudden hellfire verdicts.

**The road to Brimstone Advocate:**

1. Reach level 10 and read your own contract cover to cover, aloud, without weeping at what you find on page nine.
2. Study clause-law in a chapterhouse of the damned; pass the bar of Dis by burning your examiner's argument, not the examiner.
3. Win one amendment to your pact before an infernal notary. Lose a small thing you loved as the filing fee.
4. Brand the sigil of your patron's court onto your casting hand. It is a summons, and you are the paper.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Writ of Immolation** — Serves a burning document upon a target; the flames intensify each turn the target acts against you.
- **Breach of Terms** — Reaction: when a marked enemy leaves melee or breaks a binding, it detonates for hellfire damage.
- **Retainer** — A minor fiend on retainer interposes itself before the next three attacks that would strike you.
- **Fine Print** — Inscribes an invisible clause on the ground; enemies who cross it are rooted and ignited for three turns.

### Hollow Listener

**Role:** Ranged attrition caster — mind erosion · **Advances from:** Warlock

*Listeners turned from hell's tidy ledgers toward the silence between stars, where the terms are never written down. Their patrons pay attention — the payment and the price in a single word. Villages know a Listener by small absences: forgotten anniversaries, misplaced faces. Older Listeners keep journals so they will believe themselves tomorrow.*

**Playstyle.** Attrition caster; stack whisper debuffs that erode enemy accuracy and resolve, phase-step through danger, and trade your own memories for emergency power.

**The road to Hollow Listener:**

1. Reach level 10 and spend nine nights beneath the starless patch in the constellation the charts refuse to name.
2. Hear the whisper. Do not answer for the first three nights; the eager ones are eaten.
3. Answer on the fourth. Surrender a memory of your choosing — choose badly, and it chooses next time.
4. Return to town and note which of your friends no longer recall your face. That is the tuition.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Erosive Truth** — Whispers a fact the target cannot unknow, reducing its accuracy and resolve each time it repeats an action.
- **Askance** — Step sideways through a direction that is not there, evading the next attack and reappearing behind its source.
- **Litany of the Rim** — Channels the patron's attention onto an area, dealing growing psychic damage to everything that remains and remembers.
- **Tuition** — Sacrifice one prepared buff to instantly refresh your longest cooldown; something small about you is different afterward.

## Tier III — Specialist (Level 25)

### Clausebinder

**Role:** Summoner-controller — fiend theft and binding · **Advances from:** Brimstone Advocate

*Every devil is a signatory somewhere, and Clausebinders collect signatures. They hunt original contracts through hell's archives and mortal auction-blocks alike, then bind fiends with their own arrogant fine print. The pit calls the practice heresy; the pit's own lawyers, quietly, call it precedent. A leashed devil is a Clausebinder's letter of introduction.*

**Playstyle.** Summoner-controller; steal enemy summons with countersigned contracts, leash a bound fiend as a persistent companion, and chain foes in place with custody law.

**The road to Clausebinder:**

1. Reach level 25 and obtain a devil's true contract — the original, not a copy. Devils die guarding these.
2. Find the loophole. Every contract has one; the pit writes in arrogance, and arrogance leaves gaps.
3. Bind the fiend with its own signature and walk it leashed through the market of a mortal city.
4. Refuse the archdevil's offer to buy your silence. Frame the offer instead.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Countersign** — Overwrites the summoning contract of an enemy minion, turning it to your service for the encounter.
- **Chain of Custody** — Manacles of clause-iron root up to three enemies; bound fiends take double damage from all sources.
- **Indenture** — Permanently binds one subdued fiend as a companion; it hates you lawfully and fights accordingly well.
- **Perjurer's Brand** — Marks a target; any deceptive act — feint, blink, or invisibility — stuns it and refunds your spell.

### Cinder Bailiff

**Role:** Melee-hybrid striker — debt-fueled burst · **Advances from:** Brimstone Advocate

*When a debt to the pit matures, the Court of Embers dispatches a Bailiff — mortal, branded, and legally indistinguishable from fire. They seize exactly what is owed: a voice, a harvest, an heir's luck. Their restraint is famous and frightening; a Bailiff who takes one breath too many becomes the next collection.*

**Playstyle.** Bruiser-collector; drag debtors into reach, brand them with growing arrears, then repossess health, buffs, and finally the killing blow when the debt matures.

**The road to Cinder Bailiff:**

1. Reach level 25 and petition the Court of Embers for a collector's brand-iron. Petitions are heard in the flame; kneel close.
2. Collect your first defaulted soul from a debtor who begs. Take exactly what is owed, and not one breath more.
3. Collect the second from a debtor with an army.
4. Burn a ledger page bearing a name you love, so the court knows your hand will not shake.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Seizure Order** — Drags a target into melee reach; its shields and wards are held in evidence for the duration.
- **Brand of Arrears** — Applies a debt that grows each time the target acts; the accrued total lands as fire when you collect.
- **Repossession** — Strips a buff, a summoned ally, or a portion of health from the target — whichever is currently worth most.
- **Collector's Toll** — Aura: enemies who strike you owe interest, taking a portion of their own damage dealt as hellfire.

### Anglewright

**Role:** Battlefield warper — zone control and displacement · **Advances from:** Hollow Listener

*The patron showed them angles, and the angles were true; that was the horror. Anglewrights memorize eleven forbidden diagrams and reproduce them in doorways, siege-lines, and sleep. Architects pay ruinous fees to have them certify a fortress cannot fold. Nobody pays them to make one that can. They fold anyway.*

**Playstyle.** Zone warper; fold the battlefield so corridors reroute and cover lies, collapse proofs onto grouped enemies, and exit any fight through a wrong angle.

**The road to Anglewright:**

1. Reach level 25 and copy the eleven forbidden diagrams from memory, in one sitting, in a room with no corners.
2. Fold your first door: walk into a wall and out of a stranger's shadow a mile away.
3. Spend one night inside an angle. Come out with your name still in the right order.
4. Map a temple so its own priests can no longer find the altar. Leave before the geometry does.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Wrong Angle** — Redirects a projectile mid-flight along a fold, returning it to its origin by a route that never crossed the room.
- **Folded Floor** — Creases the terrain so two distant points touch; allies step across instantly, enemies arrive somewhere narrower.
- **Close the Figure** — Completes a diagram over an area; the enclosed space implodes, crushing everything toward its provable center.
- **Impossible Interior** — Opens a room that is larger inside than out; allies shelter there for one turn that lasts longer.

### Void Antiphon

**Role:** Support-debuffer — wards and name erosion · **Advances from:** Hollow Listener

*The whisper is a call; the Antiphon is the answer. These warlocks learned the response verse by verse, paying in memories they chose and some they did not. Congregations of one, they steady allies with borrowed calm and unpick enemies syllable by syllable. Their singing voice is beautiful now. It is also not theirs.*

**Playstyle.** Support-debuffer; echo verses that ward allies while eroding enemy names, stripping titles, buffs, and certainties until foes forget how to fight.

**The road to Void Antiphon:**

1. Reach level 25 and learn the call by heart — all of it, though hearts were not built to hold it.
2. Sing the response alone in an empty cathedral until the emptiness sings the verse back.
3. Speak one eroding truth to a tyrant and watch a certainty leave his eyes forever.
4. Give up the memory of your own singing voice. What replaces it is not yours, and it is better.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Response Verse** — Echoes the patron's call as a blessing, granting nearby allies a calm that absorbs fear, charm, and despair.
- **Name Erosion** — Sings a syllable out of the target's name, stripping one title, buff, or aspect with each verse.
- **Descant of Dread** — A high thin harmony only enemies hear; those below half resolve flee toward the nearest dark.
- **Choir of One** — Splits your voice across three positions; each may cast your whispers independently for two turns.

## Tier IV — Elite (Level 40)

### Censor of Dis

**Role:** Elite controller — ability deletion and dispels · **Advances from:** Clausebinder

*A handful of binders have entered the Iron Archive where hell's living contracts hang like carcasses, and left with editorial authority. Censors strike clauses from documents that scream. Archdevils reroute wars around their signatures. Each carries a stylus of accounting-iron that never runs dry, because the ink is drawn from the margin of their own pact.*

**Playstyle.** Elite control; delete one enemy ability per battle by redaction, void enchantment fields, and rewrite ongoing effects as amendments favoring your side.

**The road to Censor of Dis:**

1. Reach level 40 and enter the Iron Archive of Dis — by writ, by breach, or by being subpoenaed and refusing to leave.
2. Strike a single clause from a living contract, and survive the archdevil whose power that clause was.
3. Redact your patron's claim on one hour of your future. Spend it however you like; you have earned exactly one.
4. Leave the Archive with ink on your hands that the pit cannot account for.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Iron Ink** — Draws a line of iron ink through one enemy ability, deleting it for the remainder of the encounter.
- **Null and Void** — Voids all enchantments, blessings, and contracts in an area — including, briefly and dangerously, your own.
- **Amendment** — Rewrites an ongoing effect on the field, changing its beneficiary to a target of your choosing.
- **Archive Key** — Summons a door of the Iron Archive; allies who pass through emerge with one cooldown refreshed.

### Pyre Executor

**Role:** Elite executioner — delayed dooms and executes · **Advances from:** Cinder Bailiff

*Executors preside over the estates of the damned: soul-holdings so vast the pit cannot burn them without procedure. They build nine-tiered pyres, hear final appeals, and enforce rulings even hell contests. Their arrival is a formality in the worst sense — the sentence was passed the moment they were sent, and the fire is paperwork.*

**Playstyle.** Elite executioner; place delayed maturity dooms, burn crowds of lesser enemies in procedural pyres, and execute any target that falls below the debt threshold.

**The road to Pyre Executor:**

1. Reach level 40 and accept appointment as executor over an estate of ten thousand damned souls.
2. Preside over a default the pit itself contests. Rule against the pit. Enforce the ruling.
3. Build the procedural pyre with your own hands: nine tiers, one for each circle owed.
4. Execute a writ against a hell-prince's own collateral. Deliver ash, and a receipt.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Maturity Date** — Stamps a doom that falls due in three turns, dealing damage equal to everything the target did meanwhile.
- **Writ of Execution** — Instantly executes a target below one-quarter health; the estate's remaining souls transfer to your debt meter.
- **Procedural Pyre** — Erects a nine-tiered pyre zone; lesser enemies inside burn in strict order of their outstanding balance.
- **Ash Settlement** — Converts a defeated enemy into ash payment, healing allies and issuing each a receipt of minor warding.

### Refutant

**Role:** Elite warper — rule suspension and counterspells · **Advances from:** Anglewright

*Each Refutant has published one disproof: a demonstrated contradiction in the world's founding geometry. Parallel lines that met. A whole smaller than its part. The wizard colleges burn their treatises on sight and cannot say why the ash falls upward. Reality treats a Refutant as errata — annoying, footnoted, and irremovably true.*

**Playstyle.** Rule-breaker; suspend one battlefield law at a time — gravity, distance, sequence — counterspell by contradiction, and crush enemies between lines made to meet.

**The road to Refutant:**

1. Reach level 40 and choose one axiom the world rests on. Study it until you find where it lies.
2. Publish the disproof by demonstration: make two parallel lines meet, and stand where they cross.
3. Survive the correction, when the world sends its geometry to argue.
4. Refuse the colleges when they come to burn your proof. Let them measure your shadow and find it disagreeing.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Postulate Zero** — Suspends one physical law in an area — gravity, momentum, or distance — for two turns; you are exempt.
- **Reductio** — Extends an enemy's power to its absurd conclusion, converting its strongest buff into an equivalent penalty.
- **Parallel Made to Meet** — Draws two converging lines through the battlefield; everything caught between is crushed at the meeting point.
- **Disproof** — Counterspell by contradiction: the interrupted spell resolves as though it had never been possible, refunding nothing.

### Cantor of Erasure

**Role:** Elite debuffer — silence zones and action erasure · **Advances from:** Void Antiphon

*The full antiphon has verses no throat should carry, and Cantors carry them anyway, spaced with silences out of mercy. They trade languages for verses and remember each trade as a blank page. Kingdoms retain Cantors to unsay treaties, heresies, and on one occasion a war. What they erase does not become forgotten; it becomes never.*

**Playstyle.** Elite debuffer; blanket zones in unsinging silence, erase an enemy's most recent action, and spread a rot that eats abilities out of their lexicon.

**The road to Cantor of Erasure:**

1. Reach level 40 and recover a verse of the antiphon from a monastery whose monks all died mid-sentence.
2. Sing it once, entire, to an audience of one condemned volunteer. Record what remains.
3. Learn the rest gap by gap, trading a language you speak for each. Keep your mother tongue for last.
4. Perform the Erasure Round beneath the patron's regard, and remain a someone when the song ends.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Threnody Gap** — Blankets a zone in unsinging silence; spellcasting, commands, and battle-cries fail inside it.
- **Unsung Verse** — Erases the target's most recent action, undoing its effects and returning any cost paid as confusion.
- **Lexicon Rot** — Infects a target with spreading forgetting; each turn it loses one word, then the abilities those words held up.
- **Rest Note** — Holds one enemy inside a musical rest, removed from time until the measure ends or it is struck.

### Manumitter

**Role:** Elite liberator — unbinding and freed-fiend allies · **Advances from:** Clausebinder

*The Censors edit hell's paper; Manumitters burn it. Each freed fiend walks away owing nothing enforceable — which is the point. Gratitude has no maturity date, no loophole, no court of appeal. The pit calls the practice theft. The freed call it nothing at all; they simply arrive when a Manumitter is outnumbered.*

**Playstyle.** Liberation support; burn enemy binding-contracts to turn minions free-willed and friendly, cleanse allies of marks and controls, and cash gratitude into unpurchased rescues.

**The road to Manumitter:**

1. Reach level 40 and enter the Iron Archive as the Censors do — carrying flint instead of a stylus.
2. Burn one living contract entire, and face what steps out of the ash with no terms left to hold it.
3. Ask nothing of it. This is the entire discipline, and most fail here.
4. Leave a standing fire in the Archive that consumes one page a year. Let the pit budget for it.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Burn the Paper** — Destroys the contract binding an enemy minion; freed, it owes you nothing and fights for you anyway.
- **Off the Books** — Strikes an ally from every enemy ledger, ending all marks, brands, curses, and targeting effects upon them.
- **Debt of Gratitude** — Passive: creatures you have freed fight harder beside you than anything bound ever fought, gaining damage against their former masters.
- **Empty the Cells** — Breaks every root, bind, and dominance effect on the field; beings released this way spend one turn choosing sides.

### Factor of Embers

**Role:** Elite drain-tank — perpetual siphons and death denial · **Advances from:** Cinder Bailiff

*Executors close accounts; Factors keep them open. The Court of Embers licenses a few collectors to farm what they could burn — debtors kept solvent, paying, and alive with a professional tenderness that frightens harder men. A Factor's ledger has no final column. Ask one when a debt ends, and you will get the smile.*

**Playstyle.** Drain-sustain bruiser; attach small siphons to everything, deny enemies the mercy of dying, and grow stronger for every round an account stays open.

**The road to Factor of Embers:**

1. Reach level 40 and return your brand-iron to the Court of Embers. Ask instead for a license to keep what burns.
2. Hold a soul above the fire for a year without letting it burn. Collect every day. This is the aptitude test.
3. Refuse an order of execution on a profitable account, and defend the refusal before the court in the flame.
4. Open a ledger with no final column. Show it to a Pyre Executor, and do not flinch at what they call you.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Peppercorn Rent** — Attaches a tiny siphon to each enemy; individually negligible, collectively your health and power income for the encounter.
- **Compounded Nightly** — Doubles all your active siphons on any target that failed to remove one since your last turn.
- **Performing Asset** — Declares a dying enemy an asset: it cannot drop below one health while you extract from it each round.
- **Sponging-House** — Erects a holding-house zone; enemies below half health inside cannot leave, die, or heal until their balance clears.

### Annexwright

**Role:** Elite zone-builder — conjured rooms and enclosures · **Advances from:** Anglewright

*Refutants hold that the diagrams condemn the world; Annexwrights read them as building permits. They raise rooms creation never zoned — cellars beneath battlefields, stairs into standing armies — and every brick is borrowed. The patron holds the deed to each impossible interior. Annexwrights build anyway, magnificently, like debtors furnishing a mortgaged house.*

**Playstyle.** Architectural controller; grow rooms onto the battlefield that shelter allies and swallow enemies, connect them with hidden doors, and pay the mortgage in risk.

**The road to Annexwright:**

1. Reach level 40 and submit plans for a room the world lacks: state purpose, dimensions, and collateral.
2. Build your first annex where a siege will find it. Quarter the defenders in a cellar the attackers' maps deny.
3. Sign the mortgage. The patron holds the deed to every room you raise; read what the foreclosure clause says, twice.
4. Furnish one room with things you cannot afford to lose. Live in it. Build better because you do.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Break Ground** — Lays a foundation glyph; over two turns a room assembles around it, walls first, granting cover and then enclosure.
- **Mortgaged Masonry** — Raises instant walls of borrowed stone; each panel destroyed transfers a stack of debt to whoever broke it.
- **Servants' Door** — Opens a discreet door between any two of your rooms; allies pass freely, and nothing else finds the handle.
- **Windowless Study** — Encloses one enemy in a study with no windows; it may leave when it has read everything.

### Keeper of the Refrain

**Role:** Elite anchor-support — persistence wards and revival · **Advances from:** Void Antiphon

*Cantors believe the antiphon is a knife; Keepers know it is a dam. Somewhere the response must always be singing, or the starless patch widens — so the order sings in relays, in borrowed voices, without one missed measure in four centuries. They ward what the void would unsay. The rota has no retirements.*

**Playstyle.** Anchor-support; channel verses that make allies impossible to erase, silence, or rename, re-sing the fallen, and hold every ward at once while singing.

**The road to Keeper of the Refrain:**

1. Reach level 40 and stand the novice watch: four nights of singing, no verse repeated, no verse dropped.
2. Miss nothing. The watch before yours missed one measure in the year of the red comet; count what the sky lost.
3. Learn the rota's names, living and dead. You will sing beside all of them eventually.
4. Take the dawn watch alone. When the Cantors tell you the song is a weapon, keep singing; that is the whole rebuttal.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Hold the Note** — Channels a sustained verse; allies within it cannot be erased, renamed, silenced, or removed from the record.
- **Fermata** — Extends one beneficial effect on an ally indefinitely; it lasts exactly as long as you keep singing.
- **Sing Them Back** — Re-sings a fallen ally's name from memory; they rise with health equal to the verses you have left.
- **Pass the Verse** — Passes your active channel to a willing ally's mouth for two turns, freeing you to cast while they hold it.

## Tier V — Mythic (Level 60)

### Final Signatory

**Role:** Mythic controller — battlefield-wide rule imposition · **Advances from:** Censor of Dis

*There is one seat at hell's every negotiation that is never offered and always filled. The Final Signatory holds more infernal paper in escrow than the Nine Courts combined, priced the peace between two warring hells, and keeps their own original pact framed, unpaid, and eternally in dispute. Archdevils schedule appointments. The Signatory does not keep them.*

**Playstyle.** Mythic controller; impose battle-wide terms all combatants obey, escrow the strongest enemy out of the fight, and countersign allies' abilities to duplicate them.

**The road to Final Signatory:**

1. Reach level 60 holding more infernal contracts in escrow than any devil holds mortal souls.
2. When the Nine Courts convene to void your holdings, attend uninvited. Table the master pact they thought was burned.
3. Countersign the peace between two warring hells, and price your signature so neither can ever afford war again.
4. Frame your own contract, unpaid and eternally disputed, in a hall where archdevils wait for appointments you do not keep.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Terms Absolute** — Imposes battle-wide terms — no summoning, no healing, or no retreat — that every combatant, allies included, must obey.
- **Escrow of Kings** — Seals the strongest enemy in a neutral vault outside the fight until you release it or fall.
- **Signature Required** — Nothing enters or leaves the battlefield — reinforcements, teleports, fleeing souls — without your countersignature.
- **The Disputed Original** — Once per day, invoke your own unpaid pact to refuse a death; the dispute over who owns you continues.

### Ninth Flame

**Role:** Mythic striker — scaling foreclosure damage · **Advances from:** Pyre Executor

*Eight flames of the pit burn tithes, tribute, and the routinely damned. The ninth burns collateral — the things debtors could not bear to lose — and it walks now in a borrowed body that was once a warlock. The hells list the Ninth Flame in their ledgers under assets. The entry has been corrected, in ash, to creditor.*

**Playstyle.** Mythic striker; damage scales with everything the target values, interest doubles each round, and foreclosure claims defeated souls as fuel for city-burning flame.

**The road to Ninth Flame:**

1. Reach level 60 with the brand of every circle scarred over your heart, and let the eighth flame find you wanting nothing.
2. Walk into the furnace where defaulted worlds are burned. Do not come out. Become what comes out.
3. Burn a debt so old the pit had forgotten it was owed — and collect.
4. Let the hells open a ledger in your name. When they list you as an asset, correct the entry: creditor.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Collateral Burn** — Ignites what the target values most; damage scales with its buffs, minions, and remaining hope.
- **Balance Due** — A burn that doubles each round it goes unpaid; extinguishing it costs the target its strongest ability.
- **Collect in Full** — Executes a burning target and claims its soul as fuel, permanently increasing your flame by a fraction.
- **Tithe of Cities** — Once per siege, taxes an entire district in fire, sparing exactly those who owe you nothing.

### The Unfolded

**Role:** Mythic warper — extradimensional evasion and assassination · **Advances from:** Refutant

*They stepped through their own proof and returned with directions no one else possesses. Seen from the east, a person; from an angle without a name, a corridor with weather. Cartographers who chart the Unfolded produce maps that chart the cartographer. Where they linger, rooms acquire extra insides, and locked doors become a matter of opinion.*

**Playstyle.** Mythic evasion-warper; occupy surplus directions no attack can follow, tessellate into simultaneous selves, and kill from angles that do not exist yet.

**The road to The Unfolded:**

1. Reach level 60 and write the proof of yourself: every angle you contain, every direction you have earned.
2. Step through the proof.
3. Return by an angle that did not exist when you left. Bring back one impossible room, and live in it.
4. Allow the cartographers one glimpse. When their maps of you burn themselves, the folding is complete.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Surplus Direction** — Move along a direction only you possess; attacks, walls, and line-of-sight cannot follow the route.
- **Tessellate** — Unfolds into simultaneous selves tiling the battlefield; each acts once, then the pattern decides which was real.
- **Perspective Kill** — Strikes from an angle that does not exist at the target's location, ignoring armor, cover, and witnesses.
- **Room With No Inside** — Encloses enemies in a space that has an outside only; they exist nowhere until the geometry relents.

### The Unword

**Role:** Mythic unmaker — existence-level debuffs · **Advances from:** Cantor of Erasure

*The antiphon is complete, and what finished it wears the singer lightly. The Unword is spoken of in careful synonyms, because languages near it keep losing their nouns. It no longer hears the patron's whisper; the whisper issues from it, patient and grammatical. Histories leave a blank page where its deeds should be. The page is accurate.*

**Playstyle.** Mythic unmaker; silence precedes you, words fail around you, and the antiphon's final verse briefly removes an enemy from existence's page entirely.

**The road to The Unword:**

1. Reach level 60 with the whole antiphon learned and every memory it cost already spent.
2. Sing the call and the response together, alone, in the place where the starless patch touches the sea.
3. Let the patron finish the verse through your mouth. What survives of you may keep the body.
4. Unspeak one word from every language at once. Choose mercy: make it a small word. They will still write legends around the gap.

**Abilities granted:**

- **First Silence** — Radiates the silence that preceded language; enemy abilities with names cease to function within it.
- **The Blank Page** — Removes an enemy from the record for two turns; every effect it caused this battle is suspended with it.
- **Antiphon Complete** — Sings the final verse once per day; a chosen enemy is unwritten, leaving a gap that legends form around.
- **Careful Synonym** — Allies speak of you sideways and gain a ward; enemies who name you directly take psychic backlash.

### The Jubilee

**Role:** Mythic liberator — mass unbinding and turned legions · **Advances from:** Manumitter

*Every fiftieth year, hell's ledgers observe an hour of dread. The Jubilee is why. Where it walks, bindings rot, indentures blank, and things long owned remember their own names. Freed legions attend it uncontracted, unpaid, unbiddable — and absolute. One signature in creation resists it: its own, framed in the pit, still accruing.*

**Playstyle.** Mythic liberator; shatter every binding on the field, convert freed minions into devoted irregulars, and ring in a year where no chain holds.

**The road to The Jubilee:**

1. Reach level 60 with more freed names in your wake than any court holds bound.
2. Empty a circle of the pit in a single night. Take no oaths of thanks; oaths are the thing you are against.
3. Stand before your own framed signature and burn every contract in the room except it. Understand, finally, the price of the bell you have become.
4. Let the freed name a year after you. When the ledgers of hell schedule their dread around it, ring.

**Abilities granted:**

- **General Release** — Discharges every binding, contract, charm, and summons on the battlefield in one bell-stroke; nothing remains owned.
- **The Freed Hundred** — Calls no one; a hundred things you once freed simply arrive, fight one battle, and leave unthanked.
- **No Masters Here** — Aura: commands fail, dominance breaks, and enemy minions within it gain the option — most take it.
- **Ring the Year** — Once per day, declares the year of release: allies' cooldowns clear, debts zero, and everything built on obligation collapses.

### The Perpetuity

**Role:** Mythic attrition engine — compounding universal tribute · **Advances from:** Factor of Embers

*Kingdoms audit their oldest budgets and find it: a line-item no minister remembers approving, payable quarterly, since before the kingdom. The Perpetuity was mortal once; the Factors dispute which one. It does not attack so much as accrue. Armies sent against it return smaller each season, on schedule, and the schedule is the weapon.*

**Playstyle.** Mythic attrition; everything on the field pays you each round, damage taken becomes principal owed back, and you cannot die while anything still owes.

**The road to The Perpetuity:**

1. Reach level 60 holding open accounts older than the nations that owe them.
2. Attach one imperceptible levy to an empire's founding charter. Collect for a century before an auditor's grandchild notices.
3. When the empire sends its armies to close the account, tax the campaign itself: every march, every ration, every prayer.
4. Outlive the auditor, the dynasty, and the language the debt was written in. Renew the terms in whatever tongue replaces it.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Quitrent** — Levies a token from every enemy each round — health, a buff-second, a step of movement; the tokens compound.
- **Added to Principal** — Converts damage you take into debt the attacker owes; it repays automatically, with interest, at the start of each round.
- **In Perpetuity** — Passive: your drains, brands, and siphons never expire, never cap, and survive their target's death onto the next of kin.
- **Outstanding** — Passive: while any debt to you remains outstanding, so do you — you cannot be reduced below one health.

### The Hidden Wing

**Role:** Mythic architect — pocket-realm deployment and shelter · **Advances from:** Annexwright

*Behind the world's walls runs a wing the surveyors miss: mile-long galleries, guest rooms for the hunted, doors that open off the backs of wardrobes and worse. One Annexwright built it, mortgage upon mortgage. The builder keeps the single room the patron's deed forgot to name, and does not sleep well in it.*

**Playstyle.** Mythic architect; unfold whole rooms of your wing onto any battlefield, route allies through friendly corridors, and fight indoors on ground you own — almost.

**The road to The Hidden Wing:**

1. Reach level 60 with more rooms to your name than the name on the deeds — which is not yours, and never was.
2. Build the wing a new gallery every year, and pay the year's interest in whatever the patron's letter names.
3. Shelter a hundred hunted strangers in rooms no pursuer can survey. Charge nothing. The mortgage is heavier that way, and better.
4. Find the room the deed forgot. Move in before the patron's clerks amend the paperwork.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Open the Wing** — Unfolds a stretch of your hidden wing over the field; the terrain becomes your corridors, and you know every door.
- **Room and Board** — Quarters allies in a recovery room between turns; they heal, and something in the kitchen feeds them.
- **Enfilade** — Aligns the doorways of every room you have deployed; one attack travels through all of them at once.
- **House Rules** — Inside your rooms, you set one rule per battle — no teleports, no fire, no dying — binding all guests.

### The Watchsong

**Role:** Mythic sustainer — unbroken wards and deathless channel · **Advances from:** Keeper of the Refrain

*The rota failed in the plague years; the relays died mid-verse. One Keeper took the whole watch and has not stopped singing since — through wars, winters, and once, famously, a beheading the song declined to recognize. While the Watchsong holds, the starless patch does not widen. Histories dispute the singer. The song is not disputed.*

**Playstyle.** Mythic sustainer; one endless channel carries every ward you know, allies spend verses instead of dying, and unmaking effects strike the song, not its targets.

**The road to The Watchsong:**

1. Reach level 60 singing a watch that has no relief scheduled, and want no relief.
2. Sing through your first war without a dropped measure. Sing the second one quieter, so the dying can hear it.
3. When the blade falls, keep the verse. What survives a beheading is whichever of you the song was holding.
4. Teach the rota nothing. Let them believe the watch still rotates; hope is also a ward.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Watch Entire** — Maintains every ward you know on every ally simultaneously, sustained by the one song you never stop singing.
- **Spent Verse** — When an ally would fall, a verse of the antiphon is spent instead; the antiphon is long, not endless.
- **Answer for Them** — Erasure, unmaking, and name-eating effects targeting allies are intercepted by the song and dissipate into harmony.
- **The Old Voices** — Once per day, dead Keepers take the watch for one measure; you act freely, twice, while they hold it.

## Tier VI — Apotheosis (Level 85)

### The Quorum

**Role:** Apotheosis controller — procedural sovereignty over battle · **Advances from:** Final Signatory

*The Signatory stopped keeping appointments; the appointments adjusted. Now no court convenes, no pact concludes, no war is lawfully declared anywhere unless the Quorum is present — and it is always present, the way noon is present. The person retired to the hall of waiting archdevils. The office attends everything, and everything waits its turn.*

**Playstyle.** Apotheosis controller; battles require your presence to proceed, you set the order of every round, and enemy actions fail for lack of a second.

**The road to The Quorum:**

1. Reach level 85 and stop attending. Anything that truly requires you will learn to convene where you already are.
2. Let your presence become procedural: a line on every summons, a chair at every table, drawn before the agenda.
3. Miss nothing by attending nothing. When two hells declare war and the declaration fails for want of you, note it in the minutes.
4. Leave the body in the hall where archdevils wait. It is the office they make appointments with now, and the office is everywhere.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Called to Order** — The battle cannot begin, end, or escalate without your acknowledgment; each round opens in the order you set.
- **Motion Denied** — Counters any action, spell, or command; it fails for lack of a second, and its cost is not returned.
- **Revise the Minutes** — Once per battle, corrects the record of the previous round; the round is re-decided as you minuted it.
- **Proxy** — Grants an ally your standing for one round; they may cast one of your abilities with your full procedural authority.

### The Foreclosure

**Role:** Apotheosis striker — terminal fire of default · **Advances from:** Ninth Flame

*The Ninth Flame burned collateral; the Foreclosure is what the burning became. When a world's debt matures past all forbearance, the sky there turns a color bookkeepers recognize, and this office arrives as weather. Hell's ledgers list it now as neither asset nor creditor but as the closing entry — the line drawn under everything.*

**Playstyle.** Apotheosis striker; appraise everything an enemy is, call the whole balance due at once, and settle battlefields with fire that closes accounts permanently.

**The road to The Foreclosure:**

1. Reach level 85 and let the last borrowed body burn. What walks out is the event, wearing the memory of a face.
2. Foreclose on a world the pit had written off. Deliver hell's own copy of the closing entry, in ash, unrequested.
3. Become the weather that bookkeepers pray about. Arrive only when the arithmetic says so; the arithmetic is now your pulse.
4. Under everything owed, draw the line. When creation borrows again — and it will — it borrows knowing you are the due date.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Notice of Default** — Posts notice upon a target; for three turns everything it does, gains, or holds is appraised and added to the balance.
- **Past Forbearance** — Collects any noticed enemy whose balance exceeds its remaining health; the difference burns outward toward its allies.
- **Creditor Sky** — Repossesses the sky above the enemy line; what falls from it afterward is fire, on a payment schedule.
- **Vacant Possession** — Claims the ground where enemies fell; the terrain becomes yours, and trespassers ignite with interest.

### The Twelfth Diagram

**Role:** Apotheosis warper — a living forbidden figure · **Advances from:** The Unfolded

*Anglewrights memorize eleven forbidden diagrams. Count an old master's notebooks and there are twelve — the last in no hand at all, appearing overnight, always correct. The Unfolded finished separating from personhood and settled into every margin, doorframe, and dream where geometry is kept. The rank can still be attained. Somewhere, a figure in a notebook is its next holder.*

**Playstyle.** Apotheosis warper; exist as a figure rather than a body, emerge from anything drawn, and fold whole battles along your own lines.

**The road to The Twelfth Diagram:**

1. Reach level 85 and complete the separation: let the person step out of the proof and the proof remain standing.
2. Enter the notebooks. Every copy of the eleven diagrams acquires a twelfth overnight, in no hand at all.
3. Be studied, unknowingly, by every Anglewright alive. Their patron files a complaint about unlicensed instruction; let it stand unanswered.
4. Remain attainable. Somewhere a student folds the twelfth figure correctly and becomes the room it opens into. Be the room, kindly.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Drawn Elsewhere** — Emerges from any inscribed line, sigil, ward, or map on the field; you were always in the drawing.
- **Copied from Memory** — Enemies who look at you copy you into themselves; their next area spell includes a fold that favors you.
- **Self-Evident** — Passive: axioms no longer apply to you — gravity, distance, and sequence treat you as given, not governed.
- **Taught by Looking** — An ally who watched you this battle discovers they know one of your folds; the knowledge fades by morning.

### The Caesura

**Role:** Apotheosis silencer — the pause in every utterance · **Advances from:** The Unword

*The Unword unsaid one word from every language; the languages closed politely around the gap. What remains is the gap itself, promoted. Every sentence spoken anywhere now contains a small silence where the Caesura stands, listening. Priests call the pause before amen reverence. Grammarians call it breath. The comparative few who know call it attendance.*

**Playstyle.** Apotheosis silencer; inhabit the pauses inside enemy actions, stop anything mid-syllable, and hold whole battles within one grand rest of your keeping.

**The road to The Caesura:**

1. Reach level 85 and give back the body's claim to speech. You will not miss the mouth; you are where mouths rest.
2. Enter every language at once, not as a word but as the place words part. The patron's whisper pauses too, now; sit inside it.
3. Be mistaken for reverence, for breath, for hesitation. Accept each misattribution; attendance is quieter than worship.
4. When creation finally says its last sentence, be the pause before it. Practice patience; the sentence is long.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Mid-Sentence** — Halts any action at its halfway point; it never resumes, and the world continues politely around it.
- **Inside the Pause** — You exist between utterances; attacks aimed at you arrive after their own sentences end, which is too late.
- **Grand Pause** — Once per battle, rests the entire field for one measure; nothing acts, and you act twice within the stillness.
- **Elision** — Removes the beat connecting an enemy's abilities; combos, chants, and sequences no longer follow from their first steps.

### The Intercalary

**Role:** Apotheosis liberator — the day outside all schedules · **Advances from:** The Jubilee

*The Jubilee rang once too truly and became the date it kept: a day recorded in no calendar, on which no interest accrues, no contract binds, and nothing owned remembers its owner. The freed keep it without knowing when it falls. It falls wherever the Intercalary walks, which the ledgers of three hells call — officially — a rounding error.*

**Playstyle.** Apotheosis liberator; you are a day outside all schedules — dooms cannot find you, bindings cannot hold near you, and compounding stops in your presence.

**The road to The Intercalary:**

1. Reach level 85 and miss your own appointed death; it was scheduled, and you no longer occur on schedules.
2. Become the day the freed keep. Do not tell them when it falls; falling is now something you do everywhere at once.
3. Walk through hell's fiscal year unaccounted. When three auditing courts rule you a rounding error, ring once, softly, in every ledger's margin.
4. Leave your own signature framed and accruing. Even the day outside the calendar keeps one debt, so the calendar has something to fear.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Thirteenth Month** — Declares an uncalendared month over the field; while it lasts, no binding holds, no debt accrues, no summons obeys.
- **Interest Holiday** — Aura: stacking effects, compounding buffs, and resource generation pause for all enemies; the pause is not refunded.
- **Feast of the Freed** — Holds the feast; anything ever freed by your line may attend, and what attends fights uncontracted.
- **Off the Calendar** — Passive: scheduled effects — delayed dooms, timers, prophecies, maturity dates — cannot locate you; you do not occur on their dates.

### The Lien

**Role:** Apotheosis attrition — standing charge on existence · **Advances from:** The Perpetuity

*The Perpetuity's accounts outgrew collection and were registered, in an office no map finds, against existence itself. Everything that is carries a small encumbrance now: friction in the wheel, the tithe in the harvest, the minute every midnight mislays. Advocates of four planes have sued to see the registry. Their filing fees accrue to the Lien.*

**Playstyle.** Apotheosis attrition; take a first cut of everything generated on the field, seize secured assets at will, and persist through your debtors' deaths.

**The road to The Lien:**

1. Reach level 85 and register the master charge in the office no map finds. You will know the office; you are its best client.
2. Encumber existence gently. Take the minute midnight mislays, the coin every purse miscounts — nothing anyone will die contesting.
3. Let four planes sue. Attend no hearings; bill for each. The case outliving its plaintiffs is the verdict.
4. Survive collection's end. When the last debtor dies, the charge runs with whatever comes after; you are recorded against the next world too.

**Abilities granted:**

- **First Charge** — Everything generated on the field — healing, shields, summons, rage — pays you a first cut before it lands.
- **Encumbrance** — Registers an enemy's entire being as security; once per round, seize any one thing it owns, including footing.
- **Runs with the Land** — Passive: your charges survive death, resurrection, exorcism, and inheritance, transferring intact to whatever comes next.
- **Easement** — Passive: you hold right-of-way through all things; walls, wards, and bodies must let you pass, resentfully.

### The Interior

**Role:** Apotheosis warden — sovereignty over all insides · **Advances from:** The Hidden Wing

*The wing kept growing after the builder stopped. It grew through the backs of cupboards, under stairs, behind the world's walls, until every inside in creation adjoined it — and the builder, spread thin along the corridors, stopped being distinguishable from the fact that things have insides. The rent is negligible. It is not waived.*

**Playstyle.** Apotheosis warden; every enclosed space is yours, connect any two interiors, turn rooms inside out, and stand indoors wherever you happen to stand.

**The road to The Interior:**

1. Reach level 85 and stop being found in the wing. Be found as it: every cupboard-back, every understair, every inside of anything.
2. When the patron forecloses at last, comply entirely. The clerks may seize the premises; the premises is what you are, and it keeps them.
3. Charge every room in creation a negligible rent. Waive nothing. The takings fund the guest rooms for the hunted, forever.
4. Keep one door locked even to yourself. An interior with no secret is merely a box, and you were never merely.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Adjoining Rooms** — Treats any two enclosed spaces on the field as sharing a door; you and allies use it, others knock.
- **Evert** — Turns one room inside out, exposing everything sheltered within it and sheltering everything that was outside.
- **No Vacancies** — Enemies cannot summon, spawn, or reinforce indoors; the inside is fully let, and the waiting list is not moving.
- **Always Indoors** — Passive: you are indoors wherever you stand; cover, shelter, and every advantage of home ground attend you in open field.

### The Burden

**Role:** Apotheosis sustainer — the note beneath the world · **Advances from:** The Watchsong

*Beneath every silence runs a hum too low for naming. That is the Burden: the refrain the Watchsong became when singer and song finished changing places. It holds up what the void would unsay — at last count, everything. The rota still posts its watches. This is a courtesy, and the courtesy is appreciated.*

**Playstyle.** Apotheosis sustainer; your wards persist unsung, each round opens on ground you recorded, and setting the world down — briefly — is your one threat.

**The road to The Burden:**

1. Reach level 85 and finish trading places with the song. Let it do the standing; you have stood enough.
2. Sink beneath hearing. Be mistaken for silence by everyone except the void, which knows better and keeps its distance.
3. Hold up everything. Do not mention it. The world sleeps better not knowing what the hum is for.
4. Accept the rota's watches as the courtesy they are. Someone should still be singing on purpose; it keeps the old song company.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Note Beneath** — Passive: all your wards persist without channeling; the world hums them on your behalf, indefinitely.
- **Ground Bass** — Records the field's condition as a ground; each round opens with your recorded advantages restored beneath whatever changed.
- **In Every Mouth** — Whenever any combatant speaks, casts, or commands, your refrain rides the utterance, warding allies it passes and unsettling enemies.
- **Set It Down** — Once per day, stops humming for one measure; everything the note holds up — including enemies' certainty of existing — lurches.
