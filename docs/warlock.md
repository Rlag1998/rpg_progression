# 👁️ Warlock

> *Power on credit. The interest compounds nightly.*

**Archetype:** Arcane

Every Warlock owns a signature they cannot take back. Some signed for knowledge, some for revenge, one or two for love; all signed. Power arrives on schedule — the price is deferred, compounding, and collected in currencies no one thinks to guard. A Warlock's art is spending borrowed fire faster than the interest accrues, and reading every word before speaking any.

## The Tree

```text
Warlock (Lv 1)
├── Brimstone Advocate (Lv 10)
│   ├── Clausebinder (Lv 25) → Censor of Dis (Lv 40) → Final Signatory (Lv 60)
│   └── Cinder Bailiff (Lv 25) → Pyre Executor (Lv 40) → Ninth Flame (Lv 60)
└── Hollow Listener (Lv 10)
    ├── Anglewright (Lv 25) → Axiombreaker (Lv 40) → The Unfolded (Lv 60)
    └── Void Antiphon (Lv 25) → Cantor of Erasure (Lv 40) → The Unword (Lv 60)
```

## Tier I — Initiate (Level 1)

### Warlock

**Role:** Ranged caster — sustained pact damage

*Warlocks are found, not trained. A scholar who read one margin too deep. A farmer who took the fourth road at a three-way crossing. Each carries a pact instead of a pedigree, and power that answers promptly — because prompt service is how a lender keeps a client borrowing. Guilds mistrust them. Lenders never do.*

**Playstyle.** Sustained pact-bolts fueled by a debt meter that recharges when you take risks; spend health, position, or buffs as collateral for bursts of borrowed power.

**Taking the first step:**

1. Want something more than you fear the price — enough that the wanting can be heard.
2. Find the offer: a book that answers back, a crossroads with a fourth road, a dream that leaves ink on your hands.
3. Sign. With blood, with a name, with the year you would have died happy — but sign.
4. Cast your first borrowed spell, and feel the interest begin.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Umbral Levy** — Fires a bolt of borrowed shadow that deals sustained damage and refunds a sliver of your debt meter on kill.
- **Witness Mark** — Brands a target as observed by your patron, increasing your damage against it and revealing it through walls.
- **Borrowed Fire** — Spend health as collateral to double your next spell's damage; the health returns only if the spell kills.
- **Read the Terms** — Study an enemy for one turn to learn a resistance and gain a shield scaled to what you learned.

## Tier II — Adept (Level 10)

### Brimstone Advocate

**Role:** Mid-range controller — burning debuffs and bindings · **Advances from:** Warlock

*Advocates argue with hell in hell's own grammar. They study clause-law in chapterhouses whose libraries smoke faintly, and treat their pact as a live case rather than a settled debt. Devils find them insufferable and, worse, admissible. Their spellbook reads like a brief, and their fires arrive with citations attached.*

**Playstyle.** Mid-range control through burning contract sigils; brand enemies with terms, punish them for breaching, and cash accumulated clauses into sudden hellfire verdicts.

**The road to Brimstone Advocate:**

1. Reach level 10 and read your own contract cover to cover, aloud, without weeping at what you find on page nine.
2. Study clause-law in a chapterhouse of the damned; pass the bar of Dis by burning your examiner's argument, not the examiner.
3. Win one amendment to your pact before an infernal notary. Lose a small thing you loved as the filing fee.
4. Brand the sigil of your patron's court onto your casting hand. It is a summons, and you are the paper.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Writ of Immolation** — Serves a burning document upon a target; the flames intensify each turn the target acts against you.
- **Breach of Terms** — Reaction: when a marked enemy leaves melee or breaks a binding, it detonates for hellfire damage.
- **Retainer** — A minor fiend on retainer interposes itself before the next three attacks that would strike you.
- **Fine Print** — Inscribes an invisible clause on the ground; enemies who cross it are rooted and ignited for three turns.

### Hollow Listener

**Role:** Ranged attrition caster — mind erosion · **Advances from:** Warlock

*Listeners turned from hell's tidy ledgers toward the silence between stars, where the terms are never written down. Their patrons pay attention — the payment and the price in a single word. Villages know a Listener by small absences: forgotten anniversaries, misplaced faces. Older Listeners keep journals so they will believe themselves tomorrow.*

**Playstyle.** Attrition caster; stack whisper debuffs that erode enemy accuracy and resolve, phase-step through danger, and trade your own memories for emergency power.

**The road to Hollow Listener:**

1. Reach level 10 and spend nine nights beneath the starless patch in the constellation the charts refuse to name.
2. Hear the whisper. Do not answer for the first three nights; the eager ones are eaten.
3. Answer on the fourth. Surrender a memory of your choosing — choose badly, and it chooses next time.
4. Return to town and note which of your friends no longer recall your face. That is the tuition.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Erosive Truth** — Whispers a fact the target cannot unknow, reducing its accuracy and resolve each time it repeats an action.
- **Askance** — Step sideways through a direction that is not there, evading the next attack and reappearing behind its source.
- **Litany of the Rim** — Channels the patron's attention onto an area, dealing growing psychic damage to everything that remains and remembers.
- **Tuition** — Sacrifice one prepared buff to instantly refresh your longest cooldown; something small about you is different afterward.

## Tier III — Specialist (Level 25)

### Clausebinder

**Role:** Summoner-controller — fiend theft and binding · **Advances from:** Brimstone Advocate

*Every devil is a signatory somewhere, and Clausebinders collect signatures. They hunt original contracts through hell's archives and mortal auction-blocks alike, then bind fiends with their own arrogant fine print. The pit calls the practice heresy; the pit's own lawyers, quietly, call it precedent. A leashed devil is a Clausebinder's letter of introduction.*

**Playstyle.** Summoner-controller; steal enemy summons with countersigned contracts, leash a bound fiend as a persistent companion, and chain foes in place with custody law.

**The road to Clausebinder:**

1. Reach level 25 and obtain a devil's true contract — the original, not a copy. Devils die guarding these.
2. Find the loophole. Every contract has one; the pit writes in arrogance, and arrogance leaves gaps.
3. Bind the fiend with its own signature and walk it leashed through the market of a mortal city.
4. Refuse the archdevil's offer to buy your silence. Frame the offer instead.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Countersign** — Overwrites the summoning contract of an enemy minion, turning it to your service for the encounter.
- **Chain of Custody** — Manacles of clause-iron root up to three enemies; bound fiends take double damage from all sources.
- **Indenture** — Permanently binds one subdued fiend as a companion; it hates you lawfully and fights accordingly well.
- **Perjurer's Brand** — Marks a target; any deceptive act — feint, blink, or invisibility — stuns it and refunds your spell.

### Cinder Bailiff

**Role:** Melee-hybrid striker — debt-fueled burst · **Advances from:** Brimstone Advocate

*When a debt to the pit matures, the Court of Embers dispatches a Bailiff — mortal, branded, and legally indistinguishable from fire. They seize exactly what is owed: a voice, a harvest, an heir's luck. Their restraint is famous and frightening; a Bailiff who takes one breath too many becomes the next collection.*

**Playstyle.** Bruiser-collector; drag debtors into reach, brand them with growing arrears, then repossess health, buffs, and finally the killing blow when the debt matures.

**The road to Cinder Bailiff:**

1. Reach level 25 and petition the Court of Embers for a collector's brand-iron. Petitions are heard in the flame; kneel close.
2. Collect your first defaulted soul from a debtor who begs. Take exactly what is owed, and not one breath more.
3. Collect the second from a debtor with an army.
4. Burn a ledger page bearing a name you love, so the court knows your hand will not shake.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Seizure Order** — Drags a target into melee reach; its shields and wards are held in evidence for the duration.
- **Brand of Arrears** — Applies a debt that grows each time the target acts; the accrued total lands as fire when you collect.
- **Repossession** — Strips a buff, a summoned ally, or a portion of health from the target — whichever is currently worth most.
- **Collector's Toll** — Aura: enemies who strike you owe interest, taking a portion of their own damage dealt as hellfire.

### Anglewright

**Role:** Battlefield warper — zone control and displacement · **Advances from:** Hollow Listener

*The patron showed them angles, and the angles were true; that was the horror. Anglewrights memorize eleven forbidden diagrams and reproduce them in doorways, siege-lines, and sleep. Architects pay ruinous fees to have them certify a fortress cannot fold. Nobody pays them to make one that can. They fold anyway.*

**Playstyle.** Zone warper; fold the battlefield so corridors reroute and cover lies, collapse proofs onto grouped enemies, and exit any fight through a wrong angle.

**The road to Anglewright:**

1. Reach level 25 and copy the eleven forbidden diagrams from memory, in one sitting, in a room with no corners.
2. Fold your first door: walk into a wall and out of a stranger's shadow a mile away.
3. Spend one night inside an angle. Come out with your name still in the right order.
4. Map a temple so its own priests can no longer find the altar. Leave before the geometry does.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Wrong Angle** — Redirects a projectile mid-flight along a fold, returning it to its origin by a route that never crossed the room.
- **Folded Floor** — Creases the terrain so two distant points touch; allies step across instantly, enemies arrive somewhere narrower.
- **Close the Figure** — Completes a diagram over an area; the enclosed space implodes, crushing everything toward its provable center.
- **Impossible Interior** — Opens a room that is larger inside than out; allies shelter there for one turn that lasts longer.

### Void Antiphon

**Role:** Support-debuffer — wards and name erosion · **Advances from:** Hollow Listener

*The whisper is a call; the Antiphon is the answer. These warlocks learned the response verse by verse, paying in memories they chose and some they did not. Congregations of one, they steady allies with borrowed calm and unpick enemies syllable by syllable. Their singing voice is beautiful now. It is also not theirs.*

**Playstyle.** Support-debuffer; echo verses that ward allies while eroding enemy names, stripping titles, buffs, and certainties until foes forget how to fight.

**The road to Void Antiphon:**

1. Reach level 25 and learn the call by heart — all of it, though hearts were not built to hold it.
2. Sing the response alone in an empty cathedral until the emptiness sings the verse back.
3. Speak one eroding truth to a tyrant and watch a certainty leave his eyes forever.
4. Give up the memory of your own singing voice. What replaces it is not yours, and it is better.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Response Verse** — Echoes the patron's call as a blessing, granting nearby allies a calm that absorbs fear, charm, and despair.
- **Name Erosion** — Sings a syllable out of the target's name, stripping one title, buff, or aspect with each verse.
- **Descant of Dread** — A high thin harmony only enemies hear; those below half resolve flee toward the nearest dark.
- **Choir of One** — Splits your voice across three positions; each may cast your whispers independently for two turns.

## Tier IV — Elite (Level 40)

### Censor of Dis

**Role:** Elite controller — ability deletion and dispels · **Advances from:** Clausebinder

*A handful of binders have entered the Iron Archive where hell's living contracts hang like carcasses, and left with editorial authority. Censors strike clauses from documents that scream. Archdevils reroute wars around their signatures. Each carries a stylus of accounting-iron that never runs dry, because the ink is drawn from the margin of their own pact.*

**Playstyle.** Elite control; delete one enemy ability per battle by redaction, void enchantment fields, and rewrite ongoing effects as amendments favoring your side.

**The road to Censor of Dis:**

1. Reach level 40 and enter the Iron Archive of Dis — by writ, by breach, or by being subpoenaed and refusing to leave.
2. Strike a single clause from a living contract, and survive the archdevil whose power that clause was.
3. Redact your patron's claim on one hour of your future. Spend it however you like; you have earned exactly one.
4. Leave the Archive with ink on your hands that the pit cannot account for.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Iron Ink** — Draws a line of iron ink through one enemy ability, deleting it for the remainder of the encounter.
- **Null and Void** — Voids all enchantments, blessings, and contracts in an area — including, briefly and dangerously, your own.
- **Amendment** — Rewrites an ongoing effect on the field, changing its beneficiary to a target of your choosing.
- **Archive Key** — Summons a door of the Iron Archive; allies who pass through emerge with one cooldown refreshed.

### Pyre Executor

**Role:** Elite executioner — delayed dooms and executes · **Advances from:** Cinder Bailiff

*Executors preside over the estates of the damned: soul-holdings so vast the pit cannot burn them without procedure. They build nine-tiered pyres, hear final appeals, and enforce rulings even hell contests. Their arrival is a formality in the worst sense — the sentence was passed the moment they were sent, and the fire is paperwork.*

**Playstyle.** Elite executioner; place delayed maturity dooms, burn crowds of lesser enemies in procedural pyres, and execute any target that falls below the debt threshold.

**The road to Pyre Executor:**

1. Reach level 40 and accept appointment as executor over an estate of ten thousand damned souls.
2. Preside over a default the pit itself contests. Rule against the pit. Enforce the ruling.
3. Build the procedural pyre with your own hands: nine tiers, one for each circle owed.
4. Execute a writ against a hell-prince's own collateral. Deliver ash, and a receipt.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Maturity Date** — Stamps a doom that falls due in three turns, dealing damage equal to everything the target did meanwhile.
- **Writ of Execution** — Instantly executes a target below one-quarter health; the estate's remaining souls transfer to your debt meter.
- **Procedural Pyre** — Erects a nine-tiered pyre zone; lesser enemies inside burn in strict order of their outstanding balance.
- **Ash Settlement** — Converts a defeated enemy into ash payment, healing allies and issuing each a receipt of minor warding.

### Axiombreaker

**Role:** Elite warper — rule suspension and counterspells · **Advances from:** Anglewright

*Each Axiombreaker has published one disproof: a demonstrated contradiction in the world's founding geometry. Parallel lines that met. A whole smaller than its part. The wizard colleges burn their treatises on sight and cannot say why the ash falls upward. Reality treats an Axiombreaker as errata — annoying, footnoted, and irremovably true.*

**Playstyle.** Rule-breaker; suspend one battlefield law at a time — gravity, distance, sequence — counterspell by contradiction, and crush enemies between lines made to meet.

**The road to Axiombreaker:**

1. Reach level 40 and choose one axiom the world rests on. Study it until you find where it lies.
2. Publish the disproof by demonstration: make two parallel lines meet, and stand where they cross.
3. Survive the correction, when the world sends its geometry to argue.
4. Refuse the colleges when they come to burn your proof. Let them measure your shadow and find it disagreeing.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Postulate Zero** — Suspends one physical law in an area — gravity, momentum, or distance — for two turns; you are exempt.
- **Reductio** — Extends an enemy's power to its absurd conclusion, converting its strongest buff into an equivalent penalty.
- **Parallel Made to Meet** — Draws two converging lines through the battlefield; everything caught between is crushed at the meeting point.
- **Disproof** — Counterspell by contradiction: the interrupted spell resolves as though it had never been possible, refunding nothing.

### Cantor of Erasure

**Role:** Elite debuffer — silence zones and action erasure · **Advances from:** Void Antiphon

*The full antiphon has verses no throat should carry, and Cantors carry them anyway, spaced with silences out of mercy. They trade languages for verses and remember each trade as a blank page. Kingdoms retain Cantors to unsay treaties, heresies, and on one occasion a war. What they erase does not become forgotten; it becomes never.*

**Playstyle.** Elite debuffer; blanket zones in unsinging silence, erase an enemy's most recent action, and spread a rot that eats abilities out of their lexicon.

**The road to Cantor of Erasure:**

1. Reach level 40 and recover a verse of the antiphon from a monastery whose monks all died mid-sentence.
2. Sing it once, entire, to an audience of one condemned volunteer. Record what remains.
3. Learn the rest gap by gap, trading a language you speak for each. Keep your mother tongue for last.
4. Perform the Erasure Round beneath the patron's regard, and remain a someone when the song ends.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Threnody Gap** — Blankets a zone in unsinging silence; spellcasting, commands, and battle-cries fail inside it.
- **Unsung Verse** — Erases the target's most recent action, undoing its effects and returning any cost paid as confusion.
- **Lexicon Rot** — Infects a target with spreading forgetting; each turn it loses one word, then the abilities those words held up.
- **Rest Note** — Holds one enemy inside a musical rest, removed from time until the measure ends or it is struck.

## Tier V — Mythic (Level 60)

### Final Signatory

**Role:** Mythic controller — battlefield-wide rule imposition · **Advances from:** Censor of Dis

*There is one seat at hell's every negotiation that is never offered and always filled. The Final Signatory holds more infernal paper in escrow than the Nine Courts combined, priced the peace between two warring hells, and keeps their own original pact framed, unpaid, and eternally in dispute. Archdevils schedule appointments. The Signatory does not keep them.*

**Playstyle.** Mythic controller; impose battle-wide terms all combatants obey, escrow the strongest enemy out of the fight, and countersign allies' abilities to duplicate them.

**The road to Final Signatory:**

1. Reach level 60 holding more infernal contracts in escrow than any devil holds mortal souls.
2. When the Nine Courts convene to void your holdings, attend uninvited. Table the master pact they thought was burned.
3. Countersign the peace between two warring hells, and price your signature so neither can ever afford war again.
4. Frame your own contract, unpaid and eternally disputed, in a hall where archdevils wait for appointments you do not keep.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Terms Absolute** — Imposes battle-wide terms — no summoning, no healing, or no retreat — that every combatant, allies included, must obey.
- **Escrow of Kings** — Seals the strongest enemy in a neutral vault outside the fight until you release it or fall.
- **Signature Required** — Nothing enters or leaves the battlefield — reinforcements, teleports, fleeing souls — without your countersignature.
- **The Disputed Original** — Once per day, invoke your own unpaid pact to refuse a death; the dispute over who owns you continues.

### Ninth Flame

**Role:** Mythic striker — scaling foreclosure damage · **Advances from:** Pyre Executor

*Eight flames of the pit burn tithes, tribute, and the routinely damned. The ninth burns collateral — the things debtors could not bear to lose — and it walks now in a borrowed body that was once a warlock. The hells list the Ninth Flame in their ledgers under assets. The entry has been corrected, in ash, to creditor.*

**Playstyle.** Mythic striker; damage scales with everything the target values, interest doubles each round, and foreclosure claims defeated souls as fuel for city-burning flame.

**The road to Ninth Flame:**

1. Reach level 60 with the brand of every circle scarred over your heart, and let the eighth flame find you wanting nothing.
2. Walk into the furnace where defaulted worlds are burned. Do not come out. Become what comes out.
3. Burn a debt so old the pit had forgotten it was owed — and collect.
4. Let the hells open a ledger in your name. When they list you as an asset, correct the entry: creditor.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Collateral Burn** — Ignites what the target values most; damage scales with its buffs, minions, and remaining hope.
- **Balance Due** — A burn that doubles each round it goes unpaid; extinguishing it costs the target its strongest ability.
- **Collect in Full** — Executes a burning target and claims its soul as fuel, permanently increasing your flame by a fraction.
- **Tithe of Cities** — Once per siege, taxes an entire district in fire, sparing exactly those who owe you nothing.

### The Unfolded

**Role:** Mythic warper — extradimensional evasion and assassination · **Advances from:** Axiombreaker

*They stepped through their own proof and returned with directions no one else possesses. Seen from the east, a person; from an angle without a name, a corridor with weather. Cartographers who chart the Unfolded produce maps that chart the cartographer. Where they linger, rooms acquire extra insides, and locked doors become a matter of opinion.*

**Playstyle.** Mythic evasion-warper; occupy surplus directions no attack can follow, tessellate into simultaneous selves, and kill from angles that do not exist yet.

**The road to The Unfolded:**

1. Reach level 60 and write the proof of yourself: every angle you contain, every direction you have earned.
2. Step through the proof.
3. Return by an angle that did not exist when you left. Bring back one impossible room, and live in it.
4. Allow the cartographers one glimpse. When their maps of you burn themselves, the folding is complete.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Surplus Direction** — Move along a direction only you possess; attacks, walls, and line-of-sight cannot follow the route.
- **Tessellate** — Unfolds into simultaneous selves tiling the battlefield; each acts once, then the pattern decides which was real.
- **Perspective Kill** — Strikes from an angle that does not exist at the target's location, ignoring armor, cover, and witnesses.
- **Room With No Inside** — Encloses enemies in a space that has an outside only; they exist nowhere until the geometry relents.

### The Unword

**Role:** Mythic unmaker — existence-level debuffs · **Advances from:** Cantor of Erasure

*The antiphon is complete, and what finished it wears the singer lightly. The Unword is spoken of in careful synonyms, because languages near it keep losing their nouns. It no longer hears the patron's whisper; the whisper issues from it, patient and grammatical. Histories leave a blank page where its deeds should be. The page is accurate.*

**Playstyle.** Mythic unmaker; silence precedes you, words fail around you, and the antiphon's final verse briefly removes an enemy from existence's page entirely.

**The road to The Unword:**

1. Reach level 60 with the whole antiphon learned and every memory it cost already spent.
2. Sing the call and the response together, alone, in the place where the starless patch touches the sea.
3. Let the patron finish the verse through your mouth. What survives of you may keep the body.
4. Unspeak one word from every language at once. Choose mercy: make it a small word. They will still write legends around the gap.

**Abilities granted:**

- **First Silence** — Radiates the silence that preceded language; enemy abilities with names cease to function within it.
- **The Blank Page** — Removes an enemy from the record for two turns; every effect it caused this battle is suspended with it.
- **Antiphon Complete** — Sings the final verse once per day; a chosen enemy is unwritten, leaving a gap that legends form around.
- **Careful Synonym** — Allies speak of you sideways and gain a ward; enemies who name you directly take psychic backlash.
