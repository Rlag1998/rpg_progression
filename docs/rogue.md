# 🗡️ Rogue

> *Fair fights are for the buried.*

**Archetype:** Cunning

Born to alleys and taught by hunger, the Rogue learned that honor is a luxury of the well-fed. Locks open at a touch, lies sit easy on the tongue, and every shadow between two lamps is a door. Whether death arrives in a cup of wine or a city's crown vanishes overnight, somewhere a Rogue was paid — and no one saw a thing.

## The Tree

```text
Rogue (Lv 1)
├── Whisperknife (Lv 10)
│   ├── Cupbearer (Lv 25) → Hemlock Deacon (Lv 40) → Mithridate (Lv 60)
│   └── Anatomist (Lv 25) → Heartstiller (Lv 40) → Hush of Crowns (Lv 60)
└── Velvet Hand (Lv 10)
    ├── Nightlatch (Lv 25) → The Keyless (Lv 40) → Moontaker (Lv 60)
    └── Intelligencer (Lv 25) → Undercrown (Lv 40) → The Rumor (Lv 60)
```

## Tier I — Initiate (Level 1)

### Rogue

**Role:** Melee skirmisher — opportunistic strikes and escapes

*Every city breeds them: the quick-fingered children who learn doors before letters and knives before prayers. Most die young or hang younger. The ones who last become Rogues — not by ceremony, but by the quiet arithmetic of surviving one more winter than the trade usually allows.*

**Playstyle.** Strike from angles nobody watches, take what the moment offers, and be gone before the cry goes up. Positioning is everything; standing still is death.

**Taking the first step:**

1. Survive a winter in the gutters of a city that does not know your name.
2. Steal your first meal without waking the dog, the watchman, or your conscience.
3. Learn the rooftop roads until you can cross the ward blindfolded in rain.
4. Take a beating meant for a friend, and learn which debts are worth carrying.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Gutter Footwork** — Moving through enemy spaces provokes no reactions; gain bonus evasion for one round after disengaging.
- **First Cut** — Attacks against foes unaware of you deal doubled weapon damage.
- **Nimble Fingers** — Pick a lock, lift a purse, or palm an object as a free action once per turn.
- **Alley Instinct** — Sense hidden exits, tripwires, and watching eyes within thirty feet.

## Tier II — Adept (Level 10)

### Whisperknife

**Role:** Melee striker — single-target ambush burst · **Advances from:** Rogue

*The guilds mark the difference between a cutpurse and a Whisperknife the way farmers mark the difference between a fox and a wolf. These are rogues who accepted the trade's oldest contract: that some deaths are bought, and the buyer never hears the sale close.*

**Playstyle.** Study the target, choose the hour, land one silent strike that ends the conversation. Patience before violence; the fight you avoid is the one you win.

**The road to Whisperknife:**

1. Reach level 10, then bring the guild proof of a killing no watchman ever recorded.
2. Spend seven nights learning one stranger's habits, and never once be seen learning them.
3. Refuse a contract that smells wrong, and survive what the refusal costs you.
4. Bury your practice blade with the name you were born under.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Hushed Steel** — Kills made from stealth produce no sound and leave you hidden.
- **Vein Map** — Study a target for one round to learn its weakest point, increasing your next critical strike's damage.
- **Nightshade Coat** — Coat a blade with a minor toxin that dulls the victim's reflexes for three rounds.
- **Second Rooftop** — After a killing strike, immediately move half your speed without being tracked.

### Velvet Hand

**Role:** Utility infiltrator — deception, evasion, acquisition · **Advances from:** Rogue

*Velvet Hands are the ones fences greet by their working names and magistrates by their false ones. They have stopped stealing to eat and started stealing to matter — a signet here, a letter there, until half the ward owes them a favor it cannot name.*

**Playstyle.** Talk your way through the front door and pick your way through the rest. Every guard has a price, every lock a temper, every mark a vanity.

**The road to Velvet Hand:**

1. Reach level 10, then sell the same forged deed to two collectors in a single evening.
2. Enter a guildhouse through the front door under a name you invented that morning.
3. Empty a strongbox and leave something behind, so the owner doubts the theft for a year.
4. Learn who actually runs your ward, then do them one favor they never asked for.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Borrowed Face** — Assemble a convincing disguise in one minute; casual observers cannot penetrate it.
- **Featherlift** — Take a carried item from a person without triggering alarm, notice, or magical wards of lesser order.
- **Tumbler's Ear** — Open mundane locks at double speed by hearing the mechanism's flaw through the metal.
- **Small Debts** — Once per day, call in a favor: a door unbarred, a rumor planted, a witness gone traveling.

## Tier III — Specialist (Level 25)

### Cupbearer

**Role:** Damage-over-time specialist — poisons and slow ruin · **Advances from:** Whisperknife

*Cupbearers serve at high tables under honest names, and the great families pretend not to know why certain rivals sicken after certain feasts. Their craft is chemistry and etiquette in equal measure: knowing which vintage hides bitterness, and which lord tastes his wife's cup first.*

**Playstyle.** Kill slowly and from a distance of days. Layer toxins, watch the symptoms bloom, and be provably elsewhere when the physicians finally give up.

**The road to Cupbearer:**

1. Reach level 25 and grow your own garden of banes, from smuggled seed to steeped extract.
2. Serve one full season in a great house, and leave with its kitchen keys copied.
3. Drink a measured dose of your own work each dawn until your body forgives it.
4. End a man at a feast of two hundred, then stand mourning at his funeral unsuspected.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Patient Vintage** — Administer a poison that lies dormant for hours before its first symptom shows.
- **Bitter Pairings** — Combine two toxins in one dose; victims suffer both effects and resist neither.
- **Physician's Shadow** — Identify any poison, antidote, or dosage by taste, scent, or symptom alone.
- **The Kind Cup** — Feign administering an antidote to gain a target's trust; the dose deepens the affliction instead.

### Anatomist

**Role:** Precision striker — critical single-target execution · **Advances from:** Whisperknife

*Anatomists buy corpses the way scholars buy books, and for the same reason. They can name every gate in the body where life enters and leaves, and price the toll of each. Their contracts specify not merely a death, but its exact posture, expression, and hour.*

**Playstyle.** One approach, one strike, one exit. Everything else is preparation: study the body, script the moment, and never improvise unless the improvisation was rehearsed.

**The road to Anatomist:**

1. Reach level 25 and map the body's twelve fatal gates, on subjects both dead and living.
2. Study under a barber-surgeon by day for one year, and let him praise your gentle hands.
3. Take a contract specifying that the victim smile at death, and deliver it as written.
4. Stand close enough to touch your mark for an evening, then leave without striking, because the contract said Thursday.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Chart of Gates** — Ignore all armor when striking the vital points of a studied target.
- **Stillwork** — Hold perfectly motionless for hours; detection magic reads you as furniture, not flesh.
- **Measured Thrust** — Your first strike against an unaware target is always a critical hit.
- **Clean Table** — Kills leave no blood spray, struggle, or forensic sign; the scene reads as natural.

### Nightlatch

**Role:** Stealth infiltrator — mobility and objective seizure · **Advances from:** Velvet Hand

*Nightlatch is a working name passed between second-storey artists of a certain calibre — those who treat locked as an opinion and guarded as a scheduling problem. The insurers of noble vaults keep a private list of everyone suspected of holding the title; the list is itself stolen most years.*

**Playstyle.** Case the target for a week, then move through it like water finding a crack. Fight nothing you can climb past; take only what was named.

**The road to Nightlatch:**

1. Reach level 25 and take a named jewel from a vault its maker called perfect.
2. Spend a night inside a mark's bedchamber and take nothing, to prove appetite obeys you.
3. Return something you stole years ago, unseen, to a house that has since doubled its guards.
4. Leave your calling knot on the magistrate's own strongbox, tied from the inside.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Opinion of Locks** — Open any mundane lock by touch; magical locks take one quiet minute.
- **Cat's Covenant** — Climb sheer surfaces at full speed and fall forty feet without a sound or a bruise.
- **Weightless Step** — Cross pressure plates, brittle tiles, and alarm strings without triggering them.
- **The Named Thing** — Concentrate on a described object to sense its bearing within a hundred paces.

### Intelligencer

**Role:** Support controller — secrets, misdirection, leverage · **Advances from:** Velvet Hand

*Intelligencers deal in the only currency that never debases: what people would pay to keep unknown. Their archives are memorized, their informants unaware they inform, and their prices denominated in futures — a vote, a silence, a door left off the latch.*

**Playstyle.** Win fights by knowing which fights are scheduled. Gather secrets, sell misdirection, and enter every room already knowing who inside it is lying.

**The road to Intelligencer:**

1. Reach level 25 knowing one true secret about every member of the ward council.
2. Sell the same secret to three buyers, priced by what each feared it meant.
3. Cultivate an informant for a year who believes, to this day, that you are the informant.
4. Predict a riot to the hour, and be paid once for the warning and once for the cause.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Ear in the Wall** — Nominate a location you have visited; learn the substance of conversations held there each day.
- **False Ledger** — Plant a document, rumor, or remembered meeting that survives professional scrutiny.
- **Read the Room** — Learn each visible creature's strongest current desire and most recent lie.
- **Denominated in Futures** — Trade a secret to a willing creature for a favor that is magically owed.

## Tier IV — Elite (Level 40)

### Hemlock Deacon

**Role:** Attrition assassin — stacking toxins and denial · **Advances from:** Cupbearer

*The Deacons of Hemlock keep no temple, but they keep a doctrine: that some lives are questions only a quiet chemistry can answer. Kingdoms retain one the way they retain a headsman — rarely spoken of, precisely paid, and consulted only when law has run out of instruments.*

**Playstyle.** Turn whole rooms, wells, and households into instruments. Your poisons stack across days; the enemy is losing the battle before they know one has begun.

**The road to Hemlock Deacon:**

1. Reach level 40, then wait to be summoned; no one applies to the Hemlock, and it watches longer than you have been alive.
2. Compound a bane no living apothecary can name, and register its antidote with no one.
3. Poison a well and cure it by morning, to prove the doctrine is choice, not appetite.
4. Refuse a crown's gold once, so every crown afterward knows your price is not money.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Doctrine of Draughts** — Your toxins ignore purification of a lesser order; only true miracles cleanse them.
- **Congregation of Symptoms** — Foes poisoned by you spread your affliction to allies they touch.
- **Clean Departure** — Touch a dying victim to erase every trace of the compound from flesh and record.
- **Tithe of Breath** — Each foe your poisons fell restores your vigor and quickens your hands for one minute.

### Heartstiller

**Role:** Burst executioner — traceless one-strike eliminations · **Advances from:** Anatomist

*A Heartstiller's work is diagnosed, never witnessed: hale men who sat down mid-sentence and did not stand again. Physicians call it a failing of the heart, and are correct. The guild lends them out at the price of a warship, and only ever singly.*

**Playstyle.** Erase the distance, land the one touch that matters, and be a rumor before the body cools. Burst without noise; commitment without trace.

**The road to Heartstiller:**

1. Reach level 40 and still a bull's heart with two fingers, before witnesses sworn to the guild.
2. Kill a man in a locked room, and let three courts rule it a natural death.
3. Slow your own heart until the physicians pronounce you, and hear which colleagues weep.
4. Decline a contract worth a warship because the mark deserved better; the guild must fear your judgment more than your blade.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Stopped Clock** — A precise strike halts the heart; the victim dies without sound, wound, or spell-scar.
- **Between Heartbeats** — Move your full speed as a blur that mundane eyes cannot track.
- **Grave Composure** — Slow your own pulse to feign death or pass wards that detect the living.
- **Sentence Unfinished** — If your first strike kills, make a second strike against one witness within reach.

### The Keyless

**Role:** Elite infiltrator — total bypass and extraction · **Advances from:** Nightlatch

*The Keyless take their name literally: initiates surrender every key they own into the order's furnace, a promise that nothing will ever again be opened the easy way. Treasurers speak of them the way sailors speak of weather. Some vaults now hold only decoys, on actuarial advice.*

**Playstyle.** Nothing built keeps you out; the job is choosing which impossibility pays. Ghost through wards, unpick miracles, and vanish carrying the exact weight you came for.

**The road to The Keyless:**

1. Reach level 40, then rob a sitting Keyless and return the prize with your admission letter inside.
2. Take a relic from a temple whose god was watching, and leave the god uncertain.
3. Walk out of the kingdom's inescapable prison twice: once as proof, once as a favor.
4. Retire an honest locksmith with an anonymous fortune; the order pays its respects to worthy adversaries.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Unbarred** — Once per day, pass a locked door, ward, or seal as if it stood open.
- **Inventory of Ghosts** — Glance at a room and know every hidden compartment, false panel, and counterfeit within it.
- **Toll Refused** — Magical traps and alarms fail to register you as a creature at all.
- **Six-Pocket Exit** — Declare an exit route when a job begins; while on it, nothing slows you below full speed.

### Undercrown

**Role:** Battlefield manipulator — proxies, favors, command · **Advances from:** Intelligencer

*Every great city has two governments, and the honest one meets in daylight. An Undercrown holds the other: fences, beggar-lines, watch-sergeants on quiet retainers, dock tallies that round in particular directions. Kings negotiate with them through intermediaries and record the payments as sanitation.*

**Playstyle.** Fight with other people's hands. Spend favors like arrows, turn the enemy's hirelings, and shape every encounter three moves before it starts.

**The road to Undercrown:**

1. Reach level 40 holding debts from a magistrate, a dock-guild, and at least one honest priest.
2. End a gang war with three dinners and no funerals, and let both sides claim the victory.
3. Be robbed publicly, then have the goods returned with an apology before the story spreads.
4. Turn a crown tax collector into your most reliable expense.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Quiet Retainers** — In any settlement, summon armed assistance within minutes; someone nearby was already yours.
- **The Rounding Error** — Skim resources from any organization you have infiltrated; its own auditors defend the numbers.
- **Turned Coat** — Offer a visible enemy hireling a better deal mid-combat; morale decides the rest.
- **Sanitation** — Erase an event from a city's official memory: records altered, witnesses paid, ballads rewritten.

## Tier V — Mythic (Level 60)

### Mithridate

**Role:** Mythic plague-blade — inevitable attrition · **Advances from:** Hemlock Deacon

*Histories record perhaps four Mithridates, and dispute two. They are the poisoners whom poison abandoned: bodies steeped so long in bane that venom greets them as kin. Emperors have kept one imprisoned as a living antidote, and buried food-tasters who claimed the title falsely.*

**Playstyle.** You are immune to affliction and made of it. Wade through your own miasma, let enemies rot standing, and outlast anything that has blood.

**The road to Mithridate:**

1. Reach level 60 having drunk, in measured communion, every bane recorded in the guild's black herbal.
2. Survive your own funeral: let the trade bury you poisoned, and walk out of the tomb.
3. Unmake a plague you did not author, so history cannot decide whether to curse you.
4. Let a king's tasters kneel to you as the antidote of last resort, and refuse the pension.

**Abilities granted:**

- **A Thousand Draughts** — You are immune to all poisons and diseases; imbibing venom heals you instead.
- **Blood of the Bane-Eater** — Your blood is a lethal contact toxin; creatures that wound you in melee are poisoned.
- **Breath of the Garden** — Exhale a lingering miasma that fills a chamber and drifts obedient to your steps.
- **The Long Answer** — Mark a target; no rest, cure, or prayer removes your toxin until you die or relent.

### Hush of Crowns

**Role:** Mythic executioner — unstoppable single elimination · **Advances from:** Heartstiller

*Dynasties keep a private word for the Hush, and do not write it down. When succession stalls or a tyrant outlives every honest remedy, the word is spoken once, into a particular dark. No Hush has ever been caught, tried, described, or proven to exist twice.*

**Playstyle.** You are the ending other assassins pray to. Choose a name, cross any distance of guards and wards, and close the sentence history left open.

**The road to Hush of Crowns:**

1. Reach level 60 with a ledger of endings no two kingdoms can agree really happened.
2. End a tyrant that three usurpers, two armies, and one god had failed to reach.
3. Let the guild that trained you conclude, from the evidence, that you never existed.
4. Hear your work blamed on plague, treason, and heaven — and correct no one.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Spoken Name** — Mark a named target; you always know their direction, distance, and degree of fear.
- **No Witness** — Creatures who see you kill forget the seeing; records of the moment fade within a day.
- **Regicide's Patience** — Keep a sleepless, foodless vigil for seven days; while vigilant, you cannot be surprised or scried.
- **The Perfect Hour** — Once a season, declare one strike unavoidable: it ignores armor, wards, luck, and prophecy.

### Moontaker

**Role:** Mythic phantom — untouchable objective theft · **Advances from:** The Keyless

*One autumn night, in the year the ballads argue about, the moon did not rise, and the pawnshops of seven cities refused all silver. The Moontaker is whoever holds the title's proof: a theft so far beyond dispute that the world simply revised its ledgers.*

**Playstyle.** Steal concepts as easily as coin. Objectives simply come to be in your hand, and guards remember doors that were never built.

**The road to Moontaker:**

1. Reach level 60 with a collection no vault could hold, because half of it is not things.
2. Steal the moon's reflection from every still water in a province for one full night.
3. Take the coronation crown mid-ceremony and return it before the arch-priest finishes the blessing.
4. Have the insurers of seven cities jointly petition the gods about you, and be declined.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Reflected Moon** — Once per year, steal something abstract: a name, a flag's color, one night's moonrise.
- **Already Gone** — When detected, retroactively be somewhere else you could plausibly have reached this round.
- **Every Door Owed** — All doors, gates, and bridges in a city recognize you and open; none remember doing so.
- **Provenance** — Any object you hold for a full day believes it has always been yours; so do its wards.

### The Rumor

**Role:** Mythic puppeteer — total unseen orchestration · **Advances from:** Undercrown

*Scholars can prove the Rumor only by absences: treaties with a signature space left blank, portraits with one figure painted over, wars that ended for no recorded reason. Whoever holds the rank owns favors from people who have never heard of them, which is the point.*

**Playstyle.** You have no face enemies can learn. Act through cutouts and coincidences; by the time anyone suspects a design, the design has already retired.

**The road to The Rumor:**

1. Reach level 60, then burn every record that you reached any level at all.
2. End a war with one forged letter, and let two historians duel over which general wrote it.
3. Own the debts of an emperor who has never heard any of your names.
4. Be erased so completely that this codex entry is, officially, a printer's error.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Redaction** — Remove yourself from a scene retroactively; all memory, record, and divination of your presence goes blank.
- **The Standing Favor** — Once per month, anything mortal hands could plausibly have arranged simply has been.
- **Author of Coincidence** — Script an accident up to a day ahead: a slipped axle, a misdelivered letter, a guard's fever.
- **Blank Signature** — Contracts, curses, and prophecies cannot bind you; your name-space in them stays empty.
