# 🛡️ Knight

> *The vow outlasts the flesh that swore it.*

**Archetype:** Might

Every Knight begins the same way: kneeling, speaking words that cannot be unsaid. What follows differs. Some vows shine — a promise to stand between the helpless and the dark until one of them breaks. Others are grimmer bargains, taking curses and sins onto shoulders built to carry them. The armor is incidental. The Knight is whatever the oath, kept long enough, makes of a person.

## The Tree

```text
Knight (Lv 1)
├── Oathwarden (Lv 10)
│   ├── Gatesworn (Lv 25) → Warden of the Last Door (Lv 40) → The Unfallen (Lv 60)
│   └── Lanternshield (Lv 25) → Dawnward (Lv 40) → The Last Lantern (Lv 60)
└── Knight Penitent (Lv 10)
    ├── Hexdrinker (Lv 25) → The Leaden Chalice (Lv 40) → The Hollow Grail (Lv 60)
    └── Black Confessor (Lv 25) → Dolorous Guard (Lv 40) → The Nameless Vow (Lv 60)
```

## Tier I — Initiate (Level 1)

### Knight

**Role:** Tank — frontline defense, damage mitigation

*Every hold keeps a few: squires who knelt in a cold chapel and rose owning nothing but a promise. A knight-errant of the first vow guards roads, wells, and other people's harvests. Most die unremembered. The rest learn what the vow is for, and what it will eventually cost.*

**Playstyle.** Plant your feet and make the enemy's choices for them. Taunt, intercept, and trade your own health for everyone else's, one blow at a time.

**Taking the first step:**

1. Kneel a full night on chapel stone and speak the First Vow aloud to no one; the words must survive the cold.
2. Carry a wounded stranger seven miles in your own armor, and take nothing for it.
3. Stand your first watch on a wall that is attacked, and do not leave your post before relief comes.
4. Have your shield marked by a sworn knight who has watched you bleed for someone else.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Sworn Interception** — Dash to an ally within 8 meters and absorb the next attack meant for them, taking 30% reduced damage.
- **Iron Tithe** — Taunt all enemies in melee range for 4 seconds; gain 2% damage reduction per enemy affected.
- **Kneel and Rise** — Once per battle, drop to one knee to block a killing blow, then rise with 20% temporary health.
- **Vow's Edge** — Your strikes deal 15% bonus damage to any enemy currently attacking someone other than you.

## Tier II — Adept (Level 10)

### Oathwarden

**Role:** Bulwark tank — party-wide mitigation and zoning · **Advances from:** Knight

*Oathwardens are what garrisons request by name when the dark presses in. They have learned the radiant discipline: that a shield is a wall, a wall is a promise, and a promise held long enough becomes a fact. Villages measure their worth in raids that turned back without a fight.*

**Playstyle.** Anchor the line with wide guard zones and interposed shields. You win by outlasting: every second you hold, the enemy's plan rots a little further.

**The road to Oathwarden:**

1. Reach level 10, then hold a breach alone until the militia rallies behind you; a single step backward forfeits the trial.
2. Take the Radiant Oath at dawn before the people you failed to save, and name each of them without flinching.
3. Reforge your shield with a rim of temple silver and carry it unbloodied through one full season of war.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Bulwark Litany** — Chant while guarding; allies behind you take 20% less damage for as long as you have not moved.
- **The Turning Hour** — After blocking ten attacks, release a radiant pulse that staggers every enemy engaging you.
- **Shieldmeet** — Lock shields with an adjacent ally to form a two-person wall that cannot be flanked for 6 seconds.
- **Beacon Guard** — Raise your shield overhead; ranged attacks aimed at allies within 5 meters strike you instead.

### Knight Penitent

**Role:** Sacrificial tank — damage transference, self-attrition · **Advances from:** Knight

*The Dread Oath is signed, not spoken — in the petitioner's own blood, witnessed by whatever is listening. A Knight Penitent takes what others cannot survive carrying: a widow's curse, a village's plague-fear, a soldier's unforgivable order. The armor grows heavier every year, and none of the added weight is steel.*

**Playstyle.** Pull harm onto yourself deliberately — hexes, wounds, terrors — and convert the accumulating weight into grim endurance and slower, heavier blows.

**The road to Knight Penitent:**

1. Reach level 10 and sign the Dread Oath in your own blood, on a page that already bears ninety-nine names.
2. Take a dying man's confession and carry his sin a full year without telling a living soul.
3. Eat the curse laid on a stranger's child, and walk away before the family can thank you.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Take This From Them** — Transfer one curse, debuff, or wound from an ally onto yourself; gain 5% damage per affliction carried.
- **Grief Iron** — Your armor hardens with each burden you bear, granting stacking damage reduction up to 40%.
- **The Heavier Hand** — A slow overhead blow dealing bonus damage equal to twice the number of burdens you currently carry.
- **Signed in Red** — Once per day, refuse a death: survive at 1 health and accept a permanent burden stack instead.

## Tier III — Specialist (Level 25)

### Gatesworn

**Role:** Anchor tank — chokepoint denial, immovable guard · **Advances from:** Oathwarden

*A Gatesworn weds a threshold. Some hold a literal gate for decades; others carry a portable arch of blessed stone and declare any doorway theirs. Armies planning a route will pay well to learn where the Gatesworn stand, then plan a different route. Their funerals are held standing up.*

**Playstyle.** Choose the ground and become its law. Enemies pass you only diminished; you are weakest in the open field and monstrous in a doorway.

**The road to Gatesworn:**

1. Reach level 25 and hold the Weeping Gate through a full assault; the count of those who passed you must be zero.
2. Sleep thirty nights standing armored in the arch you mean to swear to, and do not fall.
3. Speak your gate's true name; you will only have learned it if the stone has accepted you.
4. Turn away a king who demanded passage you were sworn to refuse, and survive what follows.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Threshold Decree** — Sanctify a doorway or chokepoint; enemies crossing it are slowed 50% and marked for allied attacks.
- **Root of the Arch** — Become immovable: immune to knockback, pulls, and forced movement while standing on ground you have sanctified.
- **None Pass Whole** — Strike freely at any enemy moving through your reach; each hit reduces their damage by 10%.
- **The Long Stand** — For every consecutive minute you do not move, gain 5% block chance, stacking until you step away.

### Lanternshield

**Role:** Escort tank — mobile sanctuary, protective auras · **Advances from:** Oathwarden

*Where the Gatesworn hold ground, Lanternshields move it: refugees through night country, pilgrims through plague towns, children out of cities already burning. Each carries a consecrated flame in the shield's boss that has never gone out. It is said the dark itself follows a Lanternshield at a respectful distance.*

**Playstyle.** Herd the vulnerable inside your light and keep the column moving. You fight walking backward, shield high, always the last one through.

**The road to Lanternshield:**

1. Reach level 25 and lead a hundred souls through the Hollowing Fen by night, losing none. Not one.
2. Kindle your shield's flame from a temple hearth and keep it burning through storm, river, and battle for a year.
3. Walk into a plague quarter no priest will enter, and carry out whoever still breathes.
4. Refuse payment three times from people who owe you their lives; the flame dims for those who profit by it.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Carried Flame** — Your shield casts a 6-meter light; allies inside are immune to fear and take 15% less damage.
- **Rearguard Psalm** — While withdrawing from combat, you and escorted allies gain 25% movement speed and cannot be slowed.
- **Shepherd's Rebuke** — Slam your shield to blind every enemy that entered your light this turn, driving them back 4 meters.
- **No One Left** — Pull a downed ally instantly into your light and stabilize them; usable once per encounter.

### Hexdrinker

**Role:** Attrition tank — curse absorption and conversion · **Advances from:** Knight Penitent

*Hexdrinkers apprentice under witch-scarred elders, learning to open the body like a door and invite the curse inside. Their veins darken; their shadows misbehave. Cursed villages send for them the way other villages send for physicians, and pay in advance, since a Hexdrinker's gratitude is a complicated thing to hold.*

**Playstyle.** Seek out the worst magic on the field and drink it. Each swallowed hex disfigures you and empowers you in equal measure.

**The road to Hexdrinker:**

1. Reach level 25 and drink your first named curse from a witch-elder's cup, knowing she will not tell you which one.
2. Carry a plague-hex through a crowded city without letting it leap to a single soul, though it begs you in your own voice.
3. Map the scars on your body and recite what each one spared, without error, before the Penitent conclave.
4. Refuse the one curse you are not yet ready for. Knowing which one it is constitutes the trial.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Open the Vein** — Draw an active curse, hex, or damaging field into your body, ending its effect and gaining a Draught stack.
- **Blackblood Riposte** — Spend a Draught to lash out with the stolen curse, afflicting your attacker with exactly what you swallowed.
- **Scar Ledger** — Each permanent curse scar grants 3% resistance to all magic, recorded visibly on your skin.
- **The Third Swallow** — At three Draughts your strikes apply a decaying weakness; at five, you begin to hear the curses speak.

### Black Confessor

**Role:** Dread vanguard — fear control, condemned execution · **Advances from:** Knight Penitent

*Black Confessors hear what the dying cannot say to priests, and carry it so the dead may travel light. Where law fails, they execute the condemned themselves, the sin passing into the Confessor at the killing stroke. Towns are quieter after one leaves. So, forever after, is the Confessor.*

**Playstyle.** Fight under a mounting weight of borrowed guilt that terrifies enemies and fuels merciless finishing blows. Isolate the guilty, judge, and end it.

**The road to Black Confessor:**

1. Reach level 25 and hear the deathbed confession of a man you hate, then bury him gently and tell no one.
2. Carry out one lawful execution the town's own headsman refused, and stay to dig the grave.
3. Sit vigil beside a mass grave until you can name the sin that filled it, then take that sin as your own.
4. Let a guilty man you could have judged walk free, because judgment was not yours that day.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Last Rites** — Mark an enemy Condemned; your attacks against them cannot be blocked, and their allies flee the killing blow.
- **Litany of the Carried** — Recite the sins you bear; enemies within earshot suffer fear and drop their guard for 5 seconds.
- **Absolving Stroke** — Execute a Condemned enemy below 30% health, absorbing their sin as a Grief stack that empowers your next mark.
- **Weight Made Wrath** — Convert all Grief stacks into a single strike dealing damage for every confession you have ever carried.

## Tier IV — Elite (Level 40)

### Warden of the Last Door

**Role:** Siege anchor — unbreakable line, threshold command · **Advances from:** Gatesworn

*The Order of the Last Door keeps the thresholds that must never open: sealed crypt-gates, breachways to the deep dark, the doors kingdoms pretend they do not have. Each Warden knows only their own door and one other's, so no captured mind can unmake the whole. Relief never comes; it is never requested.*

**Playstyle.** Not a defender but a fact of architecture. Project fields of absolute denial, punish anything that tests the line, and never, ever be lured out.

**The road to Warden of the Last Door:**

1. Reach level 40 and be summoned; no one petitions the Last Door. The Order watches, and the Order writes to you.
2. Hold a nameless door for a year against something that knocks politely every midnight, and never once answer.
3. Learn your predecessor's fate from the scratches on the inside of the door, and take the post anyway.
4. Swear the Warden's silence: you will tell no one what you guard, including yourself, in dreams.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Door Holds** — While you stand within a sanctified threshold, enemies beyond it cannot reduce you below 1 health.
- **Masonry of Will** — Raise a 10-meter rampart of force with your health and your stubbornness; it stands for 20 seconds.
- **Toll of Passage** — Every action an enemy takes inside your threshold costs them 5% of their maximum health.
- **Bar the Door** — Slam your door: hurl all enemies out of the threshold and lock it against them for 12 seconds.

### Dawnward

**Role:** Radiant guardian — mass aegis, battlefield deliverance · **Advances from:** Lanternshield

*Dawnwards carry morning into places that have forgotten it — mines gone wrong, cities under endless eclipse, the marches where night stopped ending. Their number is never more than twelve. When a Dawnward's flame enters a dark that has held for years, things with long patience finally learn what waiting costs.*

**Playstyle.** Project overwhelming sanctuary: vast light-fields, mass shields, allies dragged back from the brink. Your presence rewrites what the dark is permitted to do.

**The road to Dawnward:**

1. Reach level 40 and carry your flame into the Sunless March, where it will either die or become something else.
2. Stand where dawn has not come in living memory, and hold your light aloft until it does.
3. Give your flame to a dying Dawnward's shield and receive theirs; twelve there are, and twelve there must remain.
4. Learn the old word for morning in the tongue the dark still fears, from the only mouth that remembers it.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Borrowed Morning** — Ignite a 20-meter field of daylight for 15 seconds; creatures of darkness within it lose all damage bonuses.
- **Aegis Unnumbered** — Grant every ally standing in your light a shield equal to 25% of your maximum health.
- **The Waking Word** — Speak the old word for dawn; all downed allies in your light rise with a third of their health.
- **Eclipse Debt** — Damage prevented by your shields accumulates; release the total as a cone of searing light.

### The Leaden Chalice

**Role:** Curse vessel — sealed affliction arsenal · **Advances from:** Hexdrinker

*When a curse is too great to break — a king's bloodline blight, a battlefield that will not stop screaming — it is given to a Leaden Chalice to hold. Their bodies are engraved with binding scripture; their footsteps are recorded in three separate archives. They are venerated, escorted, and never, ever touched.*

**Playstyle.** Walk the field as a sealed arsenal. Endure almost anything passively, and choose with great care the moment to crack a seal and let one horror out on your terms.

**The road to The Leaden Chalice:**

1. Reach level 40 and accept a Great Curse no circle of nine could break, sealed beneath your own engraved skin.
2. Endure the Scriving: a year of binding scripture cut into you, one verse each dawn, none taken under mercy of drink.
3. Walk to a war and stand between the armies until both remember what you carry. Then go home.
4. Name your successor and begin their scars yourself, for the curses must never be left ownerless.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Sealed Scripture** — Each Great Curse you carry radiates dread; enemies who strike you risk brief affliction by what leaks through.
- **Crack the First Seal** — Release one carried curse in a 12-meter burst, afflicting all enemies with its original, unweakened form.
- **Leaden Skin** — Your engraved flesh reflects 30% of magical damage back upon the caster as raw curse.
- **The Weight Rests** — Kneel and go inert for 6 seconds: untargetable and immovable while your seals fully restore.

### Dolorous Guard

**Role:** Terror arbiter — judgment burst, morale ruin · **Advances from:** Black Confessor

*The Dolorous Guard number whatever the age requires. They arrive uninvited wherever a realm's sin has grown too heavy — a massacre unpunished, a treaty signed in children's blood — and take the weight by force of judgment. Kings do not welcome them. Kings also do not stop them. No one does.*

**Playstyle.** Enter the field as a verdict. Break enemy morale in waves, isolate the guiltiest, and deliver executions that make the survivors lay down arms.

**The road to Dolorous Guard:**

1. Reach level 40 and judge a crowned head before its own court, carrying every sin you name out of the hall.
2. Bear the massacre of Cold Hollow for a decade; the Guard will weigh you by how quietly you carry it.
3. Be feared correctly: let a burning village's raiders surrender at the mere sound of your approach.
4. Give judgment against a member of the Guard itself, and be the one who carries what they carried.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Arrival** — Your first appearance in battle forces every enemy to check morale; the guilty are revealed and Marked.
- **Sentence Pronounced** — Declare a Marked enemy's sin aloud; they take 40% increased damage from all sources for 10 seconds.
- **Iron Requiem** — Advance slowly and unstoppably; each step releases carried griefs as waves of terror that break formations.
- **Take the Kingdom's Weight** — Absorb a battlefield's collective guilt: massive Grief stacks, at the cost of reduced maximum health until dawn.

## Tier V — Mythic (Level 60)

### The Unfallen

**Role:** Mythic bastion — absolute lockdown, undying stand · **Advances from:** Warden of the Last Door

*Histories disagree on whether the Unfallen is one knight or a title passed down a chain of doors. What the sieges agree on: somewhere there is a threshold that has never been crossed, and a figure in fused armor who has not sat down in a hundred years. Armies route around the legend.*

**Playstyle.** Cease to be a combatant and become terrain. Nothing crosses your line, nothing moves in your presence uninvited, and nothing kills what stands behind you.

**The road to The Unfallen:**

1. Reach level 60 and hold your door through the Sundering Siege; the histories will record that it did not open, and not your name.
2. Outlast the thing that knocks: when midnight comes and no knock follows, your watch has changed something.
3. Let your armor fuse. A smith will offer to cut you free, once, and the refusal is the rite.
4. Be mistaken for a statue by the pilgrims who come to see you, and permit it.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Terminal Threshold** — Declare a line; for 30 seconds no enemy, projectile, or spell crosses it while you draw breath.
- **The Standing Century** — You cannot die while holding your ground; lethal damage instead petrifies armor plates, shed when the battle ends.
- **Stone Remembers** — Every foe your line has ever stopped adds a permanent sliver to your block; the total no longer fits in ledgers.
- **Let Them Break** — Reflect one siege-scale attack in full back along its path, unmaking whatever engine sent it.

### The Last Lantern

**Role:** Mythic sanctuary — inviolable light, mass deliverance · **Advances from:** Dawnward

*Doctrine holds that when the final light in the world gutters, one flame will remain, carried on a shield. Refugee songs in twenty tongues describe the same figure at the rear of the column, walking backward, the dark keeping its distance like a whipped animal. No song describes them arriving. Only leaving last.*

**Playstyle.** Your light is sanctuary in law and in fact: absolute protection for everyone within it, and a slow, patient sentence for whatever waits outside.

**The road to The Last Lantern:**

1. Reach level 60 and walk the Night That Stayed from edge to edge, gathering everyone still alive inside it.
2. Bring your column home with more souls than you set out with, and account for each addition.
3. Set your flame against the oldest dark and do not blink. The witnesses say the dark blinked.
4. Be sung of in a tongue you have never heard, by people you never reached; the light travels further than you do.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Sanctum Absolute** — Allies in your light cannot be targeted, cursed, or frightened; the radius grows with each soul it shelters.
- **The Long Walk Home** — Escorted allies leave no tracks, feel no cold, and heal steadily so long as the column keeps moving.
- **Undimmed** — Your flame cannot be extinguished by any means yet recorded; each attempt blinds the attacker for 8 seconds.
- **Dawn Called Early** — Once per day, summon true daylight for one full minute, regardless of hour, depth, or curse.

### The Hollow Grail

**Role:** Mythic vessel — curse singularity, cataclysmic release · **Advances from:** The Leaden Chalice

*Scholars argue whether the Hollow Grail is a knight or a containment. Both are true. One vessel in an age drinks past the ninety-ninth seal and does not burst; what remains walks, speaks courteously, and holds enough bottled ruin to end a continent. Nations keep certain treaties specifically to avoid disturbing it.*

**Playstyle.** Carry apocalypse politely. Overwhelming passive dread, near-total immunity to magic, and the option — never yet fully taken — of opening every seal at once.

**The road to The Hollow Grail:**

1. Reach level 60 and drink the hundredth curse, the one every Reliquary before you refused, and remain courteous.
2. Sit in the treaty hall while nations negotiate around your silence; understand that your presence is the peace.
3. Hear all ninety-nine speak at once and answer none of them, for a year and a day.
4. Choose, daily, not to overturn. The histories call this the longest vigil, and it is the whole of the rank.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Ninety-Nine Seals** — You carry the great curses of an age; hostile magic cast near you is drawn in and simply kept.
- **A Sip Returned** — Pour a single measure from one seal onto an enemy; the affliction is centuries stronger than when it was bottled.
- **The Quiet Brim** — Lesser enemies cannot bring themselves to strike you; those who manage it are cursed by proximity alone.
- **Grail Overturned** — Open every seal at once. The codex does not record the effects, only the names of the craters.

### The Nameless Vow

**Role:** Mythic reckoning — inevitable judgment, terror incarnate · **Advances from:** Dolorous Guard

*Somewhere the weight of every carried sin passes a threshold, and the knight beneath it stops being anyone. The Nameless Vow has no face witnesses agree upon, no grave, no beginning. It is what arrives when a wrong has grown too vast for courts, gods, or armies — and kneels to lift it.*

**Playstyle.** You are the last consequence. Unstoppable advance, absolute terror, and judgments that end wars because no one present dares be found guilty.

**The road to The Nameless Vow:**

1. Reach level 60 and take up a sin so vast the carrying erases you; your name goes first, and you let it.
2. Judge a god's broken oath and make the judgment hold; the priesthoods will deny this happened, forever.
3. Walk out of every history that recorded you; the Dolorous Guard's own archive keeps an empty page in your honor.
4. Answer, when the last wrong asks who bears it now: no one does. And mean it.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Unspoken Sentence** — Enemies who witness your judgment of another must flee, surrender, or share the sentence.
- **No Face, All Doors** — You cannot be scried, ambushed, or barred; guilt is a door, and everyone has one.
- **Kneel to Lift It** — Absorb a calamity-scale wrong whole — a razed city's grief, a broken god's oath — and rise armored in it.
- **The Vow Outlasts** — On death, the Vow does not end; it stands the body back up and finishes the judgment first.
