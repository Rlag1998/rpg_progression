# 📖 Wizard

> *Every word costs a decade; speak carefully.*

**Archetype:** Arcane

Wizards are not born to power; they purchase it, syllable by syllable, across decades of candlelit study. To them magic is a language older than the world that speaks it, with a grammar that punishes error in blood. Where others sing or borrow, the wizard conjugates. Each year in the archives adds another word no living rival can pronounce.

## The Tree

```text
Wizard (Lv 1)
├── Convoker (Lv 10)
│   ├── Cindertongue (Lv 25) → Ashen Rhetor (Lv 40) → Voice of Ruin (Lv 60)
│   └── Graven Lector (Lv 25) → Winter Grammarian (Lv 40) → Stillword (Lv 60)
└── Deepscribe (Lv 10)
    ├── Hourwright (Lv 25) → Chronarch (Lv 40) → The Unhoured (Lv 60)
    └── Geometer (Lv 25) → Axiom-Breaker (Lv 40) → Worldscribe (Lv 60)
```

## Tier I — Initiate (Level 1)

### Wizard

**Role:** Ranged caster — versatile, preparation-driven damage

*Every tower, hedge-school, and archive keeps a few: apprentices who traded their youth for letters. A wizard at first rank owns little — a copied grimoire, a stub of candle, three words that reliably work. The world calls them scholars until the third word lands. Then it calls them carefully.*

**Playstyle.** Prepare the right spells before the fight, then spend them precisely. Fragile and slow to ready, devastating when the situation matches the page you memorized.

**Taking the first step:**

1. Learn your letters twice — once for men, once for the older things that listen beneath them.
2. Copy a dead wizard's grimoire by hand until your errors stop being errors.
3. Speak your first true word aloud, and survive being understood.
4. Swear the Candle Oath: study before sleep, accuracy before ambition, and never lend out your name.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Candle Logic** — Kindle a small, obedient flame that deals modest fire damage and never spreads beyond the word that lit it.
- **Inkbound Ward** — Trace a warding sentence midair that absorbs the next hit; the ward breaks into legible fragments enemies find briefly blinding.
- **Apprentice's Rebuttal** — Interrupt an enemy casting by mispronouncing it back at them; weak spells fizzle, strong ones merely stutter.
- **Recitation** — Recite memorized passages to restore mana over six seconds; taking damage forces you to start the page again.

## Tier II — Adept (Level 10)

### Convoker

**Role:** Ranged nuker — elemental burst and area denial · **Advances from:** Wizard

*Convokers choose the loud grammar: fire, frost, storm, and stone — the four dialects the material world speaks about itself. They are summoned to break sieges and end droughts, and paid to leave quickly. The Convocation grants the rank only to those the elements have answered by name, at least once each.*

**Playstyle.** Rotate four elemental dialects against resistances, earning short empowerments when you alternate. Loud, position-hungry casting that trades personal safety for overwhelming area damage.

**The road to Convoker:**

1. Reach level 10 and address each of the four elements by its birth-name, without flattery and without flinching.
2. Stand in a mountain storm and correct its grammar; leave only when it thunders in the tense you chose.
3. Carry a live coal, a hailstone, an unspent bolt, and a fistful of grave-earth through the Convocation doors.
4. Answer the examiners in all four dialects while your own notes burn on the lectern.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Fire's First Name** — Speak the flame's birth-name to detonate a burning zone; enemies already ignited take double the blast.
- **Bedrock Clause** — Command the ground to object, erupting a stone rampart that launches and staggers whatever stood upon it.
- **Voltaic Refrain** — Repeat a thunder-word three times; each repetition forks lightning to one additional target at growing damage.
- **Hail Imperative** — Order the sky downward, dropping a slow-marching column of frost that chills everything it crosses, then shatters.

### Deepscribe

**Role:** Battlefield controller — zones, wards, stolen tempo · **Advances from:** Wizard

*Deepscribes go down instead of out — past the elements, into the Undertext: the writing beneath the world that fixes where things are and when. Archives quarter them apart from other students. Their margins fill with runes that itch to be true, and their letters sometimes arrive before being sent.*

**Playstyle.** Shape the fight before it starts: inscribe traps, fold distances, tax enemy time. Weak in a fair exchange, so arrange that no exchange is fair.

**The road to Deepscribe:**

1. Reach level 10 and read one full page of the Undertext without your own name changing.
2. Inscribe a rune that remains true after you stop believing in it.
3. Fold a map until two cities touch, and post a letter across the crease.
4. Descend to the archive's sealed floor, and return with the same number of shadows you took down.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Footnote** — Inscribe a rune in the field's margin; the first enemy to cross it triggers a rooting annotation.
- **Folded Footnote** — Crease the space between two points and step across; the fold snaps shut behind you, clipping pursuers.
- **Borrowed Minute** — Lift sixty seconds from an enemy's future; they slow now and stagger when the debt comes due.
- **Undertext Ward** — Rewrite your outline one line deeper than reality, reducing all damage while you stand still and read.

## Tier III — Specialist (Level 25)

### Cindertongue

**Role:** Ranged striker — single-target incineration burst · **Advances from:** Convoker

*Cindertongues speak the loud grammar so precisely it stops being weather and becomes intent. Their consonants scorch parchment at ten paces; taverns serve them in stoneware. Duelists dread the Held Word — fire kept behind the teeth for hours, then released as one immaculate, unsurvivable syllable.*

**Playstyle.** Charge held-word stacks between engagements, then discharge everything into a single target. Burst windows are short and total; missing one means minutes of silent rebuilding.

**The road to Cindertongue:**

1. Reach level 25 and hold a word of fire behind your teeth from dusk to dawn without swallowing.
2. Duel a Convoker of higher rank; win with one syllable, or never speak of it.
3. Whisper to a candle across a crowded hall and light it before anyone hears you.
4. Burn your apprentice notes; a Cindertongue carries every word ignited, or not at all.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Held Word** — Bank fire on your tongue for up to an hour; its damage compounds silently until spoken.
- **Scorchword** — A single spoken bolt that ignores fire resistance in proportion to how long you have gone without speaking.
- **Fulminant Vowel** — One vowel of lightning that arcs from the burning target to whoever stands nearest in its defense.
- **Vitrify** — Fuse the ground beneath a burned enemy into glass, rooting them inside a mirror of the blast.

### Graven Lector

**Role:** Area controller — walls, slows, siege attrition · **Advances from:** Convoker

*Graven Lectors read the quiet grammar: frost that outlasts argument, stone that never learned to hurry. Fortresses retain them as living gates. It is said a Lector reads a glacier the way clerks read ledgers — aloud, line by line, back to the first winter, and is never interrupted.*

**Playstyle.** Deny ground rather than take it. Layer walls, chills, and graven clauses until enemies are fighting inside a shrinking room of your punctuation.

**The road to Graven Lector:**

1. Reach level 25 and read a glacier's strata aloud, oldest line first, until you reach a winter no calendar holds.
2. Raise a wall of ice before a siege, and still be standing behind it when the besiegers starve.
3. Carve one sentence into granite with a bare fingertip, without haste and without heat.
4. Spend a season in silence; the quiet grammar admits no student who fidgets.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Rime Parenthesis** — Enclose a zone in curved walls of ice; anything caught inside is parenthetical, and takes increased damage.
- **Graven Clause** — Carve a slowing sentence into the earth; enemies who read it — by standing on it — briefly turn to stone.
- **Hoarfrost Ledger** — Record every chill you inflict; at ten entries the ledger closes and all debtors freeze at once.
- **Weight of Strata** — Drop the memory of a mountain onto one point, crushing and half-burying whoever disagreed with you.

### Hourwright

**Role:** Tempo support — haste, delay, small rewinds · **Advances from:** Deepscribe

*Hourwrights work time the way wheelwrights work oak: measured, joined, mended. Field commanders prize them, for a battle with an Hourwright runs on their clock. They keep pocketed minutes against emergencies and pay in memories, which is why old Hourwrights ask your name twice and apologize once.*

**Playstyle.** Bank seconds in calm moments and spend them in crisis: hastening allies, delaying blows, unwinding small disasters. Everything costs time you must already have saved.

**The road to Hourwright:**

1. Reach level 25 and build a clock that keeps your pulse instead of the sun's.
2. Sit vigil over a candle burning backward, and record the hour at which it was lit.
3. Mend the shattered minute a dying Hourwright leaves you; waste nothing, not even the regret.
4. Give one year of your life to the guild's escrow, and do not ask what it is for.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Pocketed Hour** — Store an unspent hour; release it later to act freely while the field holds its breath.
- **Foregone Echo** — Replay your previous spell exactly; the echo costs no mana but ages you one visible day.
- **Clockwise Mercy** — Turn an ally's last three seconds backward, closing the wound that now has not happened yet.
- **Loitering Instant** — Stretch a single instant across a zone until arrows hang midair and oaths take minutes to finish.

### Geometer

**Role:** Zone manipulator — displacement and folded terrain · **Advances from:** Deepscribe

*Geometers measure the angles the world hopes nobody checks. Where they work, corridors shorten for friends and lengthen for creditors. The rank is conferred with a brass compass whose needle points to the bearer's true position — a courtesy, since after the third fold, few Geometers are entirely certain of it.*

**Playstyle.** Redraw the battlefield's floor plan mid-fight: fold charges into walls, open doors in distance, keep melee permanently arriving. Position is your entire vocabulary.

**The road to Geometer:**

1. Reach level 25 and walk a straight line that brings you home from the far side of the city.
2. Fold a locked room until its inside faces outward, and take nothing from it.
3. Survey a battlefield the night before, and leave one angle wrong on the enemy's map.
4. Accept the brass compass, and forgive it in advance for what it will stop knowing.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Foldstep** — Crease the floor and step over the crease, crossing thirty paces in one; the fold lingers for allies.
- **Impossible Corridor** — Bend an enemy's charge through a corner that was never there, delivering them neatly into your trap.
- **Proof by Collapse** — Demonstrate that a room's angles cannot all be true; the contradiction crushes everything inside it.
- **Squared Circle** — Inscribe a ring no straight line may cross; projectiles arrive politely at its edge and stop.

## Tier IV — Elite (Level 40)

### Ashen Rhetor

**Role:** Artillery caster — sustained multi-target ruin · **Advances from:** Cindertongue

*The Rhetors of Ash are an order of nine, rarely eleven. Cities hire them to end wars with a single address; the address is fire, delivered in paragraphs. Treaties name them as weapons, not signatories. When a Rhetor rises to speak, veteran armies discover pressing business elsewhere.*

**Playstyle.** Channel long, escalating orations of flame. Each uninterrupted sentence widens the burn; interruption is your only true counter, so guard your speaking time jealously.

**The road to Ashen Rhetor:**

1. Reach level 40 and address a city that hates you for one hour; leave it standing because you chose to.
2. Recite the Ninety Names of Fire from memory while your lectern burns beneath your notes.
3. Argue an old volcano back to sleep, or into eruption, as the order requires that year.
4. Submit your thesis in ash on stone pages, and defend it against nine Rhetors speaking at once.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Burning Thesis** — Open an argument as a channeled beam of fire that grows wider each second you remain unrefuted.
- **Ash Sermon** — Deliver a falling homily over the field; ash accumulates, smothering enemy healing and igniting on the third stanza.
- **Closing Argument** — End the discussion: every enemy below one-quarter health within earshot is summarized in flame.
- **Firebreak Diction** — Clip your own consonants to raise a ring of burnt ground no fire — theirs or yours — may cross.

### Winter Grammarian

**Role:** Fortress controller — mass denial and permanence · **Advances from:** Graven Lector

*Winter Grammarians parse motion out of sentences and armies alike. Where one stands, campaigns end in the declarative. Border kingdoms keep their names in sealed treaties, invoked only when a war must simply stop. Their corrections are permanent; thawing a Grammarian's work is heresy against grammar itself.*

**Playstyle.** Impose stillness at scale: freezes that hold, walls that outlive sieges, ground rewritten to winter. Slow, inevitable, immovable — you win by making the fight unaffordable.

**The road to Winter Grammarian:**

1. Reach level 40 and hold a mountain pass closed for a full season using nothing that melts.
2. Correct a river in spring flood, and leave it a road.
3. Parse a cavalry charge into the passive voice before the third hoofbeat.
4. Write one sentence in frost upon the order's black gate; if it survives summer, so does your candidacy.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Full Stop** — End every moving thing's sentence in a wide radius; the period is ice and lasts eight seconds.
- **Declension of Frost** — Conjugate an enemy through the cases of cold — chilled, slowed, brittle, held — one case per second.
- **Winter's Syntax** — Rewrite the ground's tense to past-perfect winter; the field becomes ice that remembers being a glacier.
- **Unmoved Clause** — Declare yourself grammatically fixed, immune to displacement and damage while you finish the current paragraph.

### Chronarch

**Role:** Tempo dominator — stolen turns and stasis · **Advances from:** Hourwright

*A Chronarch keeps court over hours the way kings keep court over counties. Debtors bring them seconds; supplicants leave younger or older, per the judgment. Armies fear their arithmetic, for battles near a Chronarch are settled before they occur, in a ledger nobody else may audit.*

**Playstyle.** Tax enemy time and disburse it to allies. Rule engagements through stolen turns, commuted deaths, and courts of stasis. Bookkeeping wins the fight before swords do.

**The road to Chronarch:**

1. Reach level 40 and audit your own life to the minute; unmake one hour and pay its full price.
2. Preside over a duel already fought, and deliver a different verdict.
3. Collect the year you escrowed as an Hourwright, with interest the guild cannot count.
4. Sit the Meridian Throne through one midnight; if dawn consents to arrive, the hour is yours to keep.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Tithe of Seconds** — Levy one second per second from every enemy in your court; your allies spend the surplus.
- **Regnal Hour** — Declare the hour yours; allies act twice within it, and interruptions are treason, punished with stasis.
- **Sentence Commuted** — Overturn a death within six seconds of its verdict; the sentence transfers, reduced, to the killer.
- **Adjournment** — Suspend the entire engagement for five seconds; only you may file motions during recess.

### Axiom-Breaker

**Role:** Rule-bender — negation and mass displacement · **Advances from:** Geometer

*Axiom-Breakers are banned in forty cities and consulted, quietly, by all forty. They disprove what battles depend on: that distance protects, that walls have insides, that ground is down. Each carries a list of postulates already broken; custom demands it be read aloud before accepting a surrender.*

**Playstyle.** Attack assumptions, not bodies. Negate signature abilities, relocate formations, invert cover. Your damage is modest; the enemy plan dying of contradictions is the win condition.

**The road to Axiom-Breaker:**

1. Reach level 40 and disprove one law of nature before witnesses; survive nature's rebuttal.
2. Escape a cell the order built to hold you, without opening it and without leaving it.
3. Publish your broken postulate in the deep archive, and duel the first three scholars who object.
4. Return the brass compass to the room where you received it, though that room no longer adjoins anything.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Counterexample** — Cite the one case where an enemy's ability fails; it fails now, and cannot be argued for twelve seconds.
- **Disproof of Distance** — Demonstrate that far is a superstition, exchanging the positions of two formations mid-maneuver.
- **Parallel Postulate** — Exist briefly along two parallel lines; both of you cast, and only one accepts the consequences.
- **Broken Compass** — Refute direction within a zone; enemy movement is redistributed by a geometry they never studied.

## Tier V — Mythic (Level 60)

### Voice of Ruin

**Role:** Cataclysm caster — battlefield-scale annihilation · **Advances from:** Ashen Rhetor

*Histories record three Voices of Ruin, and dispute the third. When a Voice speaks plainly, cartographers start over. The Third Siege of Vhal ended in one sentence — subject and verb, no survivors on the wrong side of the grammar. Nations do not fight Voices; they reschedule.*

**Playstyle.** Every cast is an event: long invocations, continental cooldowns, absolute outcomes. Played less like artillery and more like a treaty clause with a pulse.

**The road to Voice of Ruin:**

1. Reach level 60 and speak, in a place already lost, one word the world was built never to hear.
2. Unmake a mountain's name by unmaking the mountain, and leave the cartographers to argue.
3. Stand before the surviving Rhetors and say nothing; be confirmed by their silence.
4. Teach one apprentice a single safe word, so the histories may claim you showed restraint.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Ninth Vowel** — Pronounce the vowel throats were shaped to prevent; everything in the sentence's path is unwritten to ash.
- **The Last Sentence** — Speak a target's ending, subject and verb; resistance becomes a matter of punctuation, not survival.
- **Silence After** — Following any ruin you utter, a hush falls in which no enemy magic can be pronounced.
- **Weather of Embers** — Change the region's weather to fire for one minute; it falls as rain that has forgotten water.

### Stillword

**Role:** Absolute controller — stillness made terrain · **Advances from:** Winter Grammarian

*A Stillword is what remains when a Winter Grammarian stops needing sentences. The Hundred-Banner War ended when one arrived and declined it. Regions around a Stillword adopt their stillness: rivers slow, feuds pause mid-generation, avalanches wait. Historians write of them in the present tense, indefinitely.*

**Playstyle.** Impose an area of absolute quiet in which nothing hostile finishes: casts, charges, or wars. You barely act; the field simply forgets how to.

**The road to Stillword:**

1. Reach level 60 and sit one entire winter, unmoving, on the glacier you once read; let it finish your sentences.
2. Be declared dead, and correct no one.
3. Halt an avalanche mid-fall with your attention alone, then set it down gently, stone by stone.
4. Speak your final word aloud; everything after is punctuation.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Long Pause** — Halt every hostile action across the field for ten seconds; nothing ages, bleeds, or falls.
- **Unthawed Verdict** — Freeze one enemy outside all seasons; the ice is permanent until you, personally, reconsider.
- **Quiet That Holds** — An aura in which projectiles slow to a courtesy and spells must wait their turn to exist.
- **Winter, Considered** — Invite the first winter to revisit; the field becomes its memory, and only you may move at speed.

### The Unhoured

**Role:** Time controller — causality spent like coin · **Advances from:** Chronarch

*The Unhoured have stepped outside the clock entirely; calendars no longer agree on when they were born, and some now say never. They attend moments rather than inhabit them. Chronarchs rule hours; the Unhoured are owed by them. History treats each one as a rumor it cannot date.*

**Playstyle.** Spend causality directly: act inside others' moments, revoke events, arrive before your reasons do. The cost is continuity — yours, mostly, and paid without receipts.

**The road to The Unhoured:**

1. Reach level 60 and attend your own birth; introduce yourself to no one.
2. Misplace a year deliberately, and refuse every offered chance to recover it.
3. Preside over the Meridian Throne's final session, which occurred before the throne was built.
4. Let every calendar forget you, and forgive the friends who follow suit.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Meanwhile** — Act during any other creature's action, as often as your unspent past allows.
- **Never** — Revoke one event from the last minute; the world keeps the scar but not the wound.
- **Grandfather Clause** — Damage that would kill you is referred backward to an ancestral moment that declines to allow it.
- **The Uncounted Hour** — Step into the hour clocks skip; return whenever you like, from any direction, rested.

### Worldscribe

**Role:** Reality editor — rewrites the rules wholesale · **Advances from:** Axiom-Breaker

*The Undertext has authors, and once a century one of them is living. A Worldscribe carries the pen the first draft was written with — or claims to, and the claim has never survived testing by anyone who tested it. Where they annotate, geography complies. The deep archive shelves their biographies under reference.*

**Playstyle.** Edit the engagement's source: terrain, rules, outcomes. Slow, deliberate revisions with total authority. You do not fight armies; you deprecate them.

**The road to Worldscribe:**

1. Reach level 60 and find the margin where the world's first author wrote; leave your reply.
2. Carve one rune upon the world's spine, where all the folds meet, and sign it.
3. Unwrite your own broken postulates and stand in the world as it was; then choose, freely, to break them again.
4. Outlive your biography in the deep archive by editing it, once, in your own hand.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Second Edition** — Issue a correction to the last six seconds; the field reloads to your amended version.
- **Deep Revision** — Rewrite a region's terrain wholesale — mountains struck through, rivers inserted — effective immediately and retroactively.
- **Authorial Intrusion** — Step directly into any scene the Undertext is currently running, regardless of distance or wards.
- **Binding Copy** — Declare one rule of your choosing canonical for an hour; reality enforces it without appeal.
