# 🏹 Ranger

> *Where the maps end, the Ranger begins.*

**Archetype:** Cunning

Where the last road frays into deer-trail, the Ranger keeps walking. Reared on the wild borders, they read wolf-sign like scripture and trust a bowstring further than any oath. Villages hire them to find what wandered too far; kingdoms hire them to learn what is coming. They survive where maps end, and they bring back those who should not have gone.

## The Tree

```text
Ranger (Lv 1)
├── Packbound (Lv 10)
│   ├── Coursewarden (Lv 25) → Wolf-Reeve (Lv 40) → Pack Incarnate (Lv 60)
│   └── Findsman (Lv 25) → Finder of the Taken (Lv 40) → Warden of the Way Back (Lv 60)
└── Stillhunter (Lv 10)
    ├── Longeye (Lv 25) → Fatefletcher (Lv 40) → The Last Arrow (Lv 60)
    └── Lichenmask (Lv 25) → Grey Vigil (Lv 40) → The Absence (Lv 60)
```

## Tier I — Initiate (Level 1)

### Ranger

**Role:** Ranged skirmisher — traps and attrition

*Rangers begin as border children who noticed too much: the raven that meant a raiding party, the silence that meant wolves. An older warden takes them on without ceremony, teaches them tracks, wind, and hunger, then leaves them somewhere hard. The ones who walk back are Rangers. The others are lessons.*

**Playstyle.** Mark quarry and wear it down from range, laying snares along the retreat you predicted; win by knowing the ground better than anyone bleeding on it.

**Taking the first step:**

1. Walk past the last fence-post at dusk, alone, and come home with meat before anyone notices the knife is gone.
2. Apprentice yourself to a warden who has stopped counting winters, and carry their gear until they let you carry a bow.
3. Learn the forty signs of wolf, and the four that mean man.
4. Track a gut-shot stag for three days, and grant it a clean death you did not enjoy.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Quarry's Mark** — Mark one enemy; your attacks against it gain bonus damage and you sense its heading through any obstruction.
- **Wolf-Sign** — Read the ground to reveal every creature that crossed it within a day, with their number, weight, and wounds.
- **Thornfoot Snare** — Set a hidden snare that roots the first creature through it and leaves it bleeding.
- **Held Breath** — After a turn without moving, your next shot cannot be deflected and strikes a weak point.

## Tier II — Adept (Level 10)

### Packbound

**Role:** Pair skirmisher — coordinated takedowns · **Advances from:** Ranger

*Some rangers stop hunting alone the day a beast decides not to leave. The Packbound swear the old blood-oath: meat shared, wounds shared, one mind wearing two skins. Border folk trust them over any patrol, because a wolf will not lie about what it smells.*

**Playstyle.** Fight as one creature in two bodies: your companion flanks, drags, and pins while you shoot, trading senses and wounds until nothing afield is faster.

**The road to Packbound:**

1. Reach level 10, then fast alone in the high pines until a beast chooses your fire over the dark.
2. Feed it from your own ration for a full season, and never eat first.
3. Take a wound that was meant for it, and let it lick the scar.
4. Hunt one whole winter without a spoken word between you, and come back fed.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Blood-Oath** — Bind a sworn companion; you share wounds evenly and it acts on your instinct without command.
- **Two Throats** — You and your companion strike the same target within a heartbeat, and the stacked wounds bleed twice as long.
- **Borrowed Ears** — Perceive through your companion's senses at any distance, smelling fear and hearing pulses through walls.
- **Drag Down** — Your companion hamstrings a fleeing enemy and pins it prone until you arrive.

### Stillhunter

**Role:** Stealth sniper — single decisive shots · **Advances from:** Ranger

*The Stillhunter goes out alone and stops being a person for a while. They are the arrow that arrives from a hillside nobody watched, days after the target felt safe. Garrison soldiers speak of them the way sailors speak of weather: no malice in it, and no mercy either.*

**Playstyle.** Play the long game: choose your ground, vanish into it, and spend patience like coin until one perfect shot decides the encounter before it begins.

**The road to Stillhunter:**

1. Reach level 10 and lie three days in a blind of your own weaving, moving only your eyes.
2. Kill at two hundred paces something that would have killed you at two.
3. Let a search party pass within an arm's reach of you. Twice.
4. Come home, and let no one ask where you were.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Deadwood Stillness** — While motionless you cannot be detected by sight, sound, or scent, even in open ground.
- **Measured Wind** — Each turn spent aiming adds range and lethality to your next shot, without limit.
- **One Report** — Your first shot from concealment strikes for triple damage and does not reveal your position.
- **Cold Camp** — You leave no tracks, ash, or scent; nothing mortal can backtrail you.

## Tier III — Specialist (Level 25)

### Coursewarden

**Role:** Pursuit striker — anti-flight execution · **Advances from:** Packbound

*When something must not escape — a raider chief, a loosed horror, a deserter carrying plans — magistrates send for a Coursewarden. They and their beast do not fight quarry so much as spend it, running it hollow across days until it stands trembling and the ledger closes.*

**Playstyle.** Momentum is your resource: speed and damage build every turn the quarry flees, and the execution triggers when exhaustion, terror, and your companion converge.

**The road to Coursewarden:**

1. Reach level 25 and run a red hart to standing-still without loosing a single arrow.
2. Follow a raiding band over two rivers and a rockslide, then bring back their boots.
3. Refuse a bounty that asked you to stop at the border.
4. Let one quarry go, once, and understand why you never will again.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Long Chase** — Each turn your quarry moves away, you and your companion gain speed and damage; the bonus never resets.
- **Hamstring Volley** — A spread of low arrows cripples the legs of everything running in a line.
- **Red Relay** — You and your companion alternate strikes without breaking stride, each hit extending the other's reach.
- **Run to Ground** — A fleeing quarry below half strength is dropped instantly when your companion reaches it.

### Findsman

**Role:** Scout-support — detection and rescue · **Advances from:** Packbound

*When a child vanishes past the tree line, villages do not pray; they send for a Findsman. Ranger and beast pool their senses into one wide attention the wood cannot lie to. They charge little, drink less, and remember every name they carried home, and each they did not.*

**Playstyle.** Recon and retrieval: ride your companion's senses to reveal what hides, hold a trail no sorcery can wash out, and shield whoever you are bringing home.

**The road to Findsman:**

1. Reach level 25, then find the child the wood took before third nightfall, and carry her out sleeping.
2. Learn the smell of every way a person can be lost.
3. Bring back a body when you had promised a boy, and say so plainly at the door.
4. Refuse payment from a mother, once, and never speak of it.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Third Nose** — Merge senses with your companion to reveal every hidden creature, door, and grave within a bowshot.
- **Hair and Thread** — Once taken, a trail cannot be lost to water, snow, or sorcery.
- **Carry-Word** — Your companion bears a rescued creature to a spoken destination, unerring and unhindered.
- **Lantern Discipline** — Allies you escort cannot be frightened, charmed, or led astray while you watch.

### Longeye

**Role:** Extreme-range artillery — priority elimination · **Advances from:** Stillhunter

*Longeyes measure shots in hillsides. They loose at targets the eye alone cannot honestly claim, trusting wind-craft, terrain, and arithmetic older than the bow. Captains fear open windows in towns a Longeye is rumored to have passed through; the arrow arrives before the sound, and long after the grudge.*

**Playstyle.** Extreme-range elimination: spend the fight positioning and reading air, then delete the one target whose death unmakes the enemy's plan.

**The road to Longeye:**

1. Reach level 25 and cut a hanged man down at four hundred paces, in rain, with one arrow through the rope.
2. Spend a season loosing at nothing but wind-blown thistledown.
3. Miss once in front of someone who matters, and go back to the thistledown.
4. Put an arrow on a war-captain's map table, through the tent, as a letter of introduction.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Mile-Mark** — Strike a target beyond line of sight by memory of ground you have walked once.
- **Wind's Confession** — Read the air along the whole flight path; range and cover penalties do not apply to you.
- **Arcing Writ** — Loose over walls and ridgelines; the arrow descends on the target's shadow.
- **Second Shame** — If your first arrow kills, the next is nocked before the witnesses finish flinching.

### Lichenmask

**Role:** Ambush controller — concealed area denial · **Advances from:** Stillhunter

*A Lichenmask lies still long enough that the wood stops making an exception for them. Moss takes the cloak; beetles cross the bow hand. Whole armies file through passes under their count, and the tally reaches a distant desk with every button recorded and no watcher ever named.*

**Playstyle.** Area denial from perfect concealment: seed the ground with patient traps, strike only while unseen, and make the terrain itself into hostile bookkeeping.

**The road to Lichenmask:**

1. Reach level 25 and sit a fortnight in the pass while three columns march by; report every banner and every button.
2. Let moss claim your cloak before you claim a kill.
3. Go one full year without a stranger's eyes finding yours.
4. Bury your name under a flat stone, and visit it less each year.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Grown Over** — After a minute motionless, the terrain grows over you; you are undetectable until you choose otherwise.
- **Patient Root** — Your traps never expire and strengthen with each day they wait.
- **First and Only** — Attacks you make while unseen cannot be parried, dodged, or answered.
- **Swallowed Camp** — Hide a camp of six so thoroughly that hounds walk through it unknowing.

## Tier IV — Elite (Level 40)

### Wolf-Reeve

**Role:** Pack commander — encirclement and pursuit · **Advances from:** Coursewarden

*The Wolf-Reeve's warrant is older than most crowns and has no expiry. They command not one companion but a sworn pack, each wolf ranked and named, and the office answers to no magistrate once a quarry is declared. Kings have paid ransoms simply to be certain no warrant carried their name.*

**Playstyle.** Command a full pack across the whole map: encircle, cut every line of retreat, and grind a declared quarry down over days, not turns.

**The road to Wolf-Reeve:**

1. Reach level 40 and inherit a warrant from a Reeve who died mid-hunt; finish that hunt before your own name dries on the rolls.
2. Name each wolf of your pack for something you lost.
3. Refuse a king who wanted the warrant used on a debtor.
4. Close a hunt begun forty years before your birth, and burn the warrant after.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Warrant of Teeth** — Lead a pack of five sworn wolves; each can hold a separate mark.
- **Closing Ring** — Your pack forms a circle a bowshot wide; nothing inside leaves it without your leave.
- **Speech of the Old Pack** — Wild beasts within earshot obey you for one hunt, then owe you nothing.
- **No Second Winter** — A declared quarry cannot heal while any of your pack still draws breath.

### Finder of the Taken

**Role:** Warded rescuer — hostile-ground extraction · **Advances from:** Findsman

*Some places are struck from maps for good reasons, and the Finder walks into them anyway: barrow-fields that rearrange themselves, forests drowned but still growing. They are paid in advance, by custom. The feared part is not where they go; it is that the things there have started using their name.*

**Playstyle.** Endure what should not be entered: ward against lures and false voices, carry the fallen at full speed, and always know the way back out.

**The road to Finder of the Taken:**

1. Reach level 40 and enter a place struck from the maps; bring someone out still breathing.
2. Hear your true name spoken by something with no mouth, and keep walking.
3. Leave a marker at the deepest point you reached, so the next Finder aims past it.
4. Bring back one who was nine years gone, and stand quietly through the weeping.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Thread of Return** — You can always retrace your exact path, and allies holding your rope cannot be separated from it.
- **Name Under the Tongue** — You are immune to charms, lures, and voices imitating the loved or the dead.
- **Shouldered Burden** — Carry an incapacitated creature at full speed; while carried, both of you are warded from harm.
- **Salt and Iron Verge** — Draw a line the unnatural cannot cross for one night.

### Fatefletcher

**Role:** Declared executioner — inevitable single kills · **Advances from:** Longeye

*A Fatefletcher builds each arrow for one death only: the shaft cut on a named day, the fletching bound with the target's habits in mind. When the grey feather is found on a commander's pillow, sieges have lifted by breakfast. Scholars call the craft mathematics. The fletchers call it listening.*

**Playstyle.** Name the death in advance: spend turns fletching fate into a single arrow, then loose the one shot the battle has already agreed to.

**The road to Fatefletcher:**

1. Reach level 40 and fletch an arrow bearing your own name; carry it until you understand why.
2. End a siege with one shaft, and walk out through the celebrating.
3. Refuse a name you judged undeserved, and return the fee doubled.
4. Teach one apprentice the listening, then burn your notes.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Named Shaft** — Fletch an arrow against one named creature over an hour; against that creature, it cannot miss.
- **Already Loosed** — The killing shot lands before any witness registers the draw.
- **Grey Feather** — Leave a feather where a leader sleeps; their forces suffer dread and desertion until they withdraw.
- **Passes What It Must** — A named shaft ignores armor, cover, and wards standing between it and its purpose.

### Grey Vigil

**Role:** Invisible attrition — territorial denial · **Advances from:** Lichenmask

*No one has knowingly met a Grey Vigil. There are borders where warbands enter at full strength and thin, man by man, in weather and silence, with no arrow ever recovered. Other rangers find only cold blinds a decade old. The order acknowledges its dead, and no one living.*

**Playstyle.** Never be engaged: bank patience into power, remove intruders one by one when they stray, and let rumor finish what you started.

**The road to Grey Vigil:**

1. Reach level 40 and keep a border for a full year without being seen once, by enemy or friend.
2. Take the grey oath alone; if the order heard it, they will not say.
3. Turn back an army using only its own count of the missing.
4. Attend your predecessor's funeral, and leave before you are counted.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Thinning** — Each night, one intruder in your territory who strays from the others does not rejoin them.
- **No Such Watcher** — Within your sworn ground, no sense, spell, or record can establish that you exist.
- **Borrowed Weather** — Fog, rain, and dusk arrive when you need them, and side with you.
- **Quiet Ledger** — Every day you go unseen adds lethality to your first strike, without cap.

## Tier V — Mythic (Level 60)

### Pack Incarnate

**Role:** Mythic pursuit — many-bodied inescapable hunt · **Advances from:** Wolf-Reeve

*Histories from six kingdoms describe the same thing sideways: a hunt on the far borders that never quite ended, one will wearing many bodies. Wolves are part of it; so, the accounts insist, is something shaped like a ranger. When it takes a scent, sensible nations make peace to avoid being downwind.*

**Playstyle.** Act through every body at once: the pack is your reach, your senses, and your argument, and no distance or border interrupts it.

**The road to Pack Incarnate:**

1. Reach level 60 and run one quarry across three kingdoms and through the middle of a war, letting no border slow the pack.
2. Fall in the snow with the pack around you, and rise when they nose you up. Twice.
3. Give up your reflection; the pack sees you, and that is enough.
4. Let the histories name the hunt, and never the hunter.

**Abilities granted:**

- **One Will, Many Throats** — Act through every pack member simultaneously; distance between bodies is irrelevant.
- **Scent Eternal** — A marked quarry cannot rest, heal, or hide until the hunt concludes.
- **The Hunt Arrives** — Your pack emerges from whatever cover exists at the quarry's destination, having always been ahead.
- **Winter Runs With You** — Cold, dark, and hunger afflict your quarry and feed your pack.

### Warden of the Way Back

**Role:** Mythic rescuer — retrieval across final borders · **Advances from:** Finder of the Taken

*Every land tells it differently and means the same story: someone taken past all finding, and years later a figure at the gate returning them unaged, declining supper. Grave-priests preach against the tale. Mothers go on telling it, and set a lamp in the window in case the Warden is passing.*

**Playstyle.** Anchor the living and reclaim the lost: open doors out of anywhere, hold names against death, and pay tolls no one else can carry.

**The road to Warden of the Way Back:**

1. Reach level 60 and walk into the country the dead walk out of; return leading someone by the hand.
2. Learn what the toll is, and pay it without telling anyone the price.
3. Return a child to the woman who buried her, and stay for the silence afterward.
4. Leave your own lamp lit. You will not be home for a while.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Door Where None Was** — Once a day, open a way out of any place, including places that are not places.
- **Kept Name** — Hold a dying ally's name under your tongue and lead them back before dawn.
- **The Long Carry** — Carry one soul any distance, across any border, at a cost you settle later.
- **Lamp in the Window** — Anyone lost who remembers you can always be found by you.

### The Last Arrow

**Role:** Mythic artillery — war-ending single shot · **Advances from:** Fatefletcher

*The chronicles disagree on the archer and agree on the arrow: a siege that lifted mid-assault, a tyrant dead at dinner behind three walls, a war that ended with one report no one heard. Scholars argue whether it is a single bowman or an office quietly passed down. The quivers stay one arrow light either way.*

**Playstyle.** Everything is windage for one shot: spend the battle unseen and calculating, then loose the arrow the war was always waiting for.

**The road to The Last Arrow:**

1. Reach level 60 and end a war with one arrow, then let history misname the archer.
2. Decline three thrones offered by people who guessed correctly.
3. Loose at something the priests say cannot die, and make them revise.
4. Pass the office on the way it came to you: an arrow, a heading, and no names.

**Abilities granted:**

- **The Shot Foretold** — Declare a death anywhere beneath the same sky; the arrow keeps the appointment.
- **The Held Breath** — Time thins around your draw; nothing moves between anchor and loose.
- **No Wall Was Built** — The shaft ignores fortification, armor, ward, and weather standing between it and the declared.
- **Quiver of One** — You carry a single arrow; it is always the right one.

### The Absence

**Role:** Mythic ghost — unwitnessed erasure · **Advances from:** Grey Vigil

*Officially, there is no such ranger. Yet there are woods that veteran armies refuse to enter for reasons their generals cannot commit to paper, and border wars that ended because the winning side began, quietly, to be alone. Scholars call the Absence a superstition. The superstition has a body count.*

**Playstyle.** Win without ever appearing: you cannot be perceived or recorded, so enemies fight the terrain, the dark, and their own arithmetic instead.

**The road to The Absence:**

1. Reach level 60 and be declared dead by the order that trained you; attend the rite unnoticed.
2. Empty a fortress without breaching a gate or leaving an arrow.
3. Be disproven by a royal commission. Assist their research.
4. Become the reason the wood is quiet, and let quiet be the whole of your name.

**Abilities granted:**

- **Never There** — You cannot be perceived, remembered, or recorded by any means, mortal or otherwise.
- **The Unentered Wood** — Enemy forces refuse to enter ground you have claimed; orders to do so trigger desertion.
- **Death Without an Author** — Your kills present no wound, cause, or story that any investigation can hold.
- **Last Doubt** — Beings of fate and prophecy cannot include you in their accounts.
